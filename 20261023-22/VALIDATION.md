# Validation record

The automated geometry suite checked 78 built-in parameter cases (defaults, extrema, three presets, all 64 endpoint combinations and eight deterministic variations), plus 24 image/STL import cases. Independent binary-STL parsing checks nonempty finite nondegenerate faces, welded two-face edge incidence, consistent winding, positive component volume and expected millimeter bounds. Each final solid has one connected component. CSG point probes check leaf roots, primary/secondary beams and open gaps. Symmetric-difference checks verify that imports leave geometry above Y=8 mm unchanged.

Mechanism calculations bound simultaneous primary and daughter rotations of 0°, 1° and 2°: branch-to-primary separation remains over 3 mm and neighboring daughter-tip separation over 2.9 mm throughout supported ranges. These are conservative undeformed beam sweep estimates, not stress/force analysis. Primary leaf free length is approximately 14 mm; exposed daughter leaf lengths 6–10 mm; minimum leaf thickness 1.2 mm. The one-piece structure needs no pins, separate retention, assembly, running clearance or mating-fit coupon. Feather Branch is the full-size TPU calibration trial.

Browser tests download real STL after PNG, JPEG, binary STL and ASCII STL picker imports. Hashes and dimensions are compared against the exact mesh driving preview; threshold/depth/crop/content and STL shape/scale/rotation/placement changes alter exported geometry. Tests exercise presets, variations, invalid content, truncated/open/nonmanifold/concave/oversized meshes, excessive pixel/triangle counts, invalid dimensions, empty silhouette, unsafe/nonfinite transforms, unit confirmation, replace/remove/reset, per-body export, undo/redo and portable JSON import round trips. Phone tests cover 320/390 px layouts, touch orbit/pinch, source picker/download and collapsible tabbed controls with reachable export.

Machine-readable reports and screenshots are produced by the corresponding test scripts. No physical print, fatigue, force, or material test was performed. Actual return behavior depends on TPU formulation, slicing and layer adhesion; a successful geometry check is not proof of a successful physical print.
