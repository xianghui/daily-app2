## Printing status and limits

**No physical print, fit, overhang quality, locking force, friction, wear or lifecycle test has been performed.** Short inward track-roof overhangs may require calibrated removable/soluble supports; exterior lug supports may also be needed. Pocket Turn is a full-size calibration preset, not a universal tolerance guarantee. The manual bayonet is intentionally removable at entry alignment, not permanently captive. Print guidance and material assumptions are explicit in the app, ZIP and README. Unsupported STL classes are rejected, not silently repaired. All bundled inputs are synthetic; personal files stay local.
