## Scope

These are computational mesh, geometry/mechanism and browser checks, **not physical printing**. No physical print, friction, force, wear, fatigue or durability tests performed. Clearances require printer/material calibration. Upper gate is intentionally removable, not captive. The preview is an explanatory hand-guided exchange sequence, not dynamics. STL integration deliberately rejects unsupported concavities/overhangs rather than changing source geometry silently. Image conversion is bounded sampled 2D relief on a solid floor, not exact outline tracing or full 3D reconstruction.
