Pulp & Deckle — miniature paper-making mould kit card.
1. Print the connected card bed-flat at 100% mm scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers and three walls. No supports intended; inspect slicer. For smaller beds use three clean parts. Adult craft kit; not food contact or a certified toy.
2. Snip twelve accessible low runners about 1.6 mm outboard of each part; trim nubs without filing sockets, ledges or tongues. Retain mould, thin deckle ring and upright handle.
3. Calibrate with the mould and handle first. The reference measured 1.6 mm sheet and 4.5 × 1.700012 mm receiving loops. Tongue 4.4 + total allowance .10 reproduces that section. Default .15 total allowance is a proposed printer/material fit, not universal. The handle lowers from above into two rear-wing through-slots; shoulders rest on the mould and tips stop .05 mm above bed. Bond both fixed seats with filament-compatible water-resistant adhesive and cure fully.
4. Supply fine synthetic papermaking screen. Measure its thickness; set Screen allowance to that value (.15–.40 mm). Cut it to the displayed seat width and height minus twice Deckle side clearance, with corners matching the pocket. Lay it taut over the open mould aperture onto the recessed ledge, then glue its entire perimeter to that ledge outside the paper opening. Do not glue across the opening or clog the screen. The external screen is NOT printed or included in STL. Its stiffness, mesh count, tension and wet adhesion need a physical trial.
5. Lower the removable deckle ring vertically over the screen, within the outer registration lip. Side clearance is PER SIDE; socket allowance is TOTAL. The ring stays loose under gravity, not snap-locked. Screen allowance reserves its thickness below the deckle; the modelled deckle top is flush only when your actual screen matches this allowance. Do not glue the deckle. The ledge remains at least 1.2 mm thick and the registration lip is 2.4 mm wide.
6. Supply a shallow basin, recycled-paper pulp, felt/couching cloth and sponge. Place deckle on the screened mould, submerge the aperture horizontally in well-mixed pulp, lift slowly and let water drain through the external mesh. Hold the frame level with both hands; the small handle helps orientation, not heavy loads. Lift the deckle straight up, invert the mould onto damp felt, sponge away water and gently peel the mould/screen away. Press/dry the sheet separately. Clean and dry parts promptly; avoid hot water. Physical sheet formation, release, drainage and wet strength are untested.
7. Change opening width/height, border, grip height, finger opening and three outer profiles. The paper aperture and removable ring opening remain registered. The handle is outside the screened pocket; no mechanism is fused by engraving.
8. Personalization engraves the front signature lug only. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG/text/icons use a bounded 20×20 closed heightfield Boolean subtraction; bright holes stay unengraved on a continuous backing. This is sampled 2D-to-relief, not full 3D reconstruction. ASCII/binary STL exact triangles form a real Boolean cutter; only validated closed outward convex broad-flat solids are supported. Unsupported input is rejected, never convex-hulled or claimed repaired. Art depth .4–.6 mm, backing ≥3.0 mm.
9. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables 865769; https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card ; CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Original measured-interface reconstruction; raw source mesh not redistributed. Stepped screen ledge, removable deckle, handle and carrier are new.
10. No physical print, assembly, papermaking, screen retention, wet strength or drainage was tested. Computational fit/tab/insertion checks do not prove craft performance. External screen, adhesive, pulp, basin, cloth and sponge are required.
