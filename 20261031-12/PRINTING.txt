Petal & Preserve — six-part ventilated botanical drying cassette kit.
1. Print bed-flat, 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. No supports intended; inspect slicer. Adult craft miniature, small parts, not food contact. Physical printing and botanical preservation untested.
2. Snip 24 low runners 1.6 mm outside the part edges. Trim only nubs; keep sockets, key slots and shoulder surfaces intact. Keep two perforated plates, two identical shouldered posts and two headed removable cross-keys.
3. Calibrate the base/post and post/key pair first. Reference mesh sheet 1.6 mm, receiving slots 4.5 × 1.700012 mm. Tenon/key width 4.4 plus TOTAL .10 allowance recreates that section. Default .15 TOTAL allowance is proposed, material/printer dependent. Sheet/key thickness and allowances change real interfaces; do not scale individual finished parts in the slicer.
4. Insert post tenons DOWN into the two lower-plate sockets. Broad shoulders rest on its top; tenon tips stop .05 mm above the bed. Glue only these fixed seats with filament-compatible adhesive and cure completely. Each broad post rises through a larger lid slot. No screws, bearings or magnets required.
5. Sandwich a small dry leaf or thin petal between two clean absorbent-paper sheets and flat blotter/cardboard pads. Keep pads wholly inside the central area between posts; keep vents and slots clear. The Paper stack space setting reserves 4–8 mm including paper, pads and specimen. It is NOT an automatic clamp or force setting.
6. Lower the lid vertically along both posts. Pass each cross-key horizontally through the post window from the front; the wide head stops forward overtravel. Key underside sits .20 mm above the nominal lid top, limiting lifting but NOT squeezing the specimen. Keys have clearance fits, not positive latches: they can slide out if tilted. Wrap TWO soft external elastic bands around the plate ends and over the key heads to supply gentle compression and retain keys. Select bands and pad thickness by physical trial; never force or bend the thin posts. Remove bands, slide keys forward, then lift the lid to reopen.
7. Replace damp paper regularly. Drying time depends on specimen, humidity, paper and ventilation; no timing, archival-quality or biological result is promised. Avoid wet storage, hot water and overtight bands. External paper/blotters, two soft elastic bands and adhesive are required; none is included in printable meshes.
8. Three real plate profiles and presets, size, vent bores, stack space, material-dependent joint clearance and runner dimensions alter actual geometry. Marks engrave only the outboard base signature lug, away from specimen, post seats and vents. STL carries no colors or units.
9. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG/text/icons use a bounded 20×20 closed heightfield Boolean engraving. Bright holes remain unengraved on continuous backing. 2D-to-relief conversion, not automatic 3D reconstruction. Validated ASCII/binary STL exact triangles form a real Boolean cutter; only one closed outward convex broad-flat solid is supported. Unsupported concave/open/nonmanifold input is rejected, not hulled or claimed repaired.
