Stereo & Scene — five-part binocular stereoscope kit card.
1. Print bed-flat, 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. No supports intended; inspect your slicer. Default card 250 × 190 mm: use the five clean parts on smaller beds. Adult craft / optical experiment, not a vision test. Physical print, assembly and optical focus untested.
2. Snip 20 accessible low tabs 1.6 mm outside part boundaries. Keep ocular panel, rear picture panel, central separator and two identical cross feet. Trim nubs, never functional slots. Calibrate the separator/foot cross-lap before printing the card. Default .15 mm TOTAL allowance (.075 per side) is proposed, not universal. Source sheet 1.6 mm and 1.700012 mm receiving sections inform sheet + .10 fit; this kit uses redesigned cross-laps, not original source tabs.
3. Stand the central separator lengthwise. Lower both feet over its bottom-open slots from BELOW (or invert the separator during assembly), using complementary top-open foot slots. Align bottom edges on the desk. Lower the ocular and picture panels into the two top-open separator slots; their complementary bottom-open slots meet at half height. Open insertion paths are verified. These are gravity-seated clearance joints, NOT clips, snap locks or travel mechanisms. A tiny spot of filament-compatible adhesive is optional for permanent assembly, not supplied or mandatory. Lift panels off before disassembly.
4. Supply TWO identical 25 mm diameter positive magnifying lenses, approximately +10 diopters / 100 mm focal length, plus removable tape or glue dots. The 18–22 mm clear apertures leave 1.5–3.5 mm radial rim under a 25 mm lens; tape each lens on the OUTSIDE/front of the ocular panel, not inside the separator. No integral lens retention is claimed. Lens center separation 56–64 mm is the viewing spacing, not a medical prescription. Do not use if uncomfortable; never point lenses at sun or intense light.
5. Tape an upright paper stereo pair onto the inward/front face of the rear picture panel, centered at lens height (60% of panel height). Left-eye scene goes left, right-eye scene right. Keep center seam against separator; never wrap paper over joints. The central opaque separator reduces cross-view. Print the included stereo-pair.svg at its written millimeter size, or make your own pair. Lens spacing and image scale must match. Personalization imports engrave a protected central brow mark only; they are not auto-generated stereo images.
6. Image plane separation is measured between panel MIDPLANES (72–88 mm). Actual inner distance equals separation minus half the two panel thicknesses. With approximate f=100 mm lenses and image inside focal distance, a virtual image may be viewable; lens thickness, eye relief, prescription and image disparity affect focus. No optical fusion, universal focus or 3D depth result is guaranteed. Move the whole viewer relative to your eyes; choose another supported separation and reprint if needed. Stop immediately if viewing strains your eyes.
7. Three real panel silhouettes and presets, plane distance, lens spacing/apertures, sheet and cross-lap clearance all change exported geometry. Image/STL/text/icon art engraves the central brow above the slot, away from optics and cross-laps. STL carries neither preview color nor units.
8. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG use bounded 20×20 threshold / grayscale closed-heightfield Boolean engraving. Bright holes stay unengraved with continuous backing. This is 2D-to-relief, not single-photo 3D reconstruction. Closed convex broad-flat ASCII/binary STL triangles are an exact Boolean engraving cutter; unsupported concavity, open/nonmanifold meshes and overhangs are rejected, never hulled or silently repaired.

All files stay local; no physical tests claimed.
