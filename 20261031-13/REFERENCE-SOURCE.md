Local files were discovered first; actual Keycap template and Weave diamond-link were parsed read-only and rejected for sheet-kit interfaces. The public reference was freshly downloaded to /tmp/stereo-scene-reference.stl, parsed and sectioned before final geometry construction. Download URL, hash, dimensions, components and sections are in reference-measurements.json. Reproduce with reference-inspect.py and trimesh/numpy/matplotlib. No raw reference STL bundled.

Original measured 1.6 mm sheet and 1.700012 mm receiving direction inform our sheet/clearance parameters. Source 4.5 mm slot length is not claimed as a new joint dimension; cross-laps are explicitly redesigned. Stereo optical spacing, complementary panel/foot laps and twenty-tab carrier are original. Source is nonwatertight, not silently repaired. No endorsement implied. Physical and optical testing unperformed.
