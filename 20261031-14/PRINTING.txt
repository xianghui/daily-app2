Lift & Reeving — five-part fixed-pulley teaching kit.
1. Print all parts bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale in PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Inspect the small sloped sheave groove in your slicer. The vertical axle prints head-down. Default card 180 × 180 mm. No physical print, load, rotation, cord tracking or assembly test has been performed.
2. Snip 20 low accessible runners, 1.6 mm outside the five part envelopes. Trim nubs, never bores, groove or tongue. Source 1.6 mm sheet and 1.700012 mm receiving direction inform the tower mortise: sheet + .15 mm TOTAL allowance by default (source .10 mm, proposed default .15). This is independent of the .25 mm radial journal gap. Calibrate both on your printer before printing the whole kit.
3. Seat the bottom tower tongue into the sole through-mortise, flush with its underside, and bond with filament-compatible adhesive. Let cure. This is a clearance seat, not a snap fit. The tower stands at the rear of the sole. Hold the sole on the desk during use; this is NOT load-bearing or lifting-rated equipment.
4. Slide the headed axle through the tower from its REAR, head against the rear tower face. Bond ONLY the axle/tower seat and let cure. Slide the sheave onto the forward journal. It has a radial running gap and equal axial gaps on each side; no glue in the wheel bore. Slide the keeper ring onto the free end, leaving the specified front axial gap, and bond it to the shaft. Both axle head and keeper overlap the wheel bore. Keeper retention depends on cured adhesive; the unglued ring is not positively captured. Keep adhesive away from the running wheel.
5. Supply 1.0–1.4 mm soft cord. Drape it over the top groove, with one end down each side, without crossing the tower. Hold the base and gently pull one free end; the other rises. A FIXED pulley reverses direction at nominal 1:1 travel, not mechanical advantage. No weights, high speed, fingers in pinch points, hanging loads, lifting people or animals. The open groove guides cord but does not enclose it; cord can derail. Not a toy for small children (small parts / cord).
6. Three real tower silhouettes, sheave diameter, tower rise/width, sheet, thickness and running fit change printable geometry. Image/STL/text/icon art engraves only the front sole landing, away from the mortise. STL contains no preview colors or unit metadata.
7. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG use a bounded 20×20 closed-heightfield Boolean engraving. Bright holes remain unengraved on continuous backing. This is 2D-to-relief, not automatic full-3D photo reconstruction. One outward-wound closed convex, flat-footprint ASCII/binary STL becomes an exact Boolean engraving cutter. Unsupported concavity, open/nonmanifold meshes and overhangs are rejected, never hulled or silently repaired.
8. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card , CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Only measured sheet/socket and low breakaway carrier construction informs the reconstruction. Pulley, journal, adhesive keeper, grooved rim and teaching interaction are new. No raw third-party STL bundled. All input processing is local. Computational geometry checks are not physical validation.
