Axis & Filter — three-part crossed-polarizer study kit.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale in PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Default card 210 × 150 mm. No supports intended. Physical printing, assembly and optical behavior have NOT been tested.
2. Snip twelve low runners, 1.6 mm outside deck/tower and 3.5 mm outside the analyzer. Trim nubs, never tongues, bores or film seats. The reference kit measures 1.6 mm sheet and 1.700012 mm receiving thickness. Our default .15 mm TOTAL mounting allowance and thicker .4–.6 mm runners are proposed printer-dependent starting values, not universal fits. Print a small tongue/socket calibration first.
3. Lower the two tower tongues into deck mortises with its circular film pocket facing the front deck edge. Shoulders rest on the deck; tongue undersides end flush. Bond both clearance seats with filament-compatible adhesive and let cure. These are not snap fits; fixed-frame retention depends on glue.
4. Supply TWO separate linear-polarizing film discs, .1–.25 mm thick, cut to clear-aperture diameter + 4 mm. Example default: two 32 mm discs. Both frames have a .4 mm-deep circular pocket with independent TOTAL diametric film allowance (.3 mm default) and a 2 mm nominal annular support shelf. Align the first film axis to the fixed tower, then tape only the outer film margins to the frame. Tape the second film into the paddle, with its axis initially parallel to the handle. Film and tape are external consumables, not printable parts. Do not mistake ordinary transparent film for linear polarizer.
5. Hold the analyzer by its lower handle in FRONT of the fixed window and look through both toward a diffuse room light or white display. Keep fingers off the film. Rotate the analyzer by hand around the shared sight line to explore changes in transmission. The circular apertures remain aligned; there is no printed pivot or captive joint. Preview angles are illustrative hand positions, not a mechanical constraint or brightness simulation. Film-axis accuracy, ambient polarization and alignment affect the result. NEVER view the sun, lasers or intense lamps; this is NOT protective eyewear or a calibrated instrument.
6. Tower width/rise, aperture and film-pocket fit change actual solids. Three deck/tower profiles and real presets; image/SVG/text/icons use local 20×20 closed-heightfield Boolean deck engraving, away from both mortises. Bright holes stay unengraved on continuous backing. This is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D reconstruction. Verified convex broad-flat-bottom ASCII/binary STL becomes an exact engraving cutter with explicit mm confirmation. Unsupported concavity, cavities, open/nonmanifold meshes or overhangs are rejected, never hulled/repaired. STL stores no colors or units.

All imports stay local. Computational fit/mesh checks do not prove optical transmission or physical performance.
