Rasp & Rhythm — three-part tactile scraper kit.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale in PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Default card 210 × 126 mm; all parts start at Z=0. No supports intended. No physical printing, assembly, tactile force, sound or fatigue test performed.
2. Snip twelve low runners, at the indicated landings (1.6 mm outside deck/rail, 3.5 mm outside the tapered wand). Trim nubs, never teeth or mounting tongues. The source kit measures 1.6 mm sheet and 1.700012 mm receiving thickness. Default mounting clearance is .15 mm TOTAL, proposed rather than universal. Print a small tongue/socket test in your material before the whole kit.
3. Lower the rail vertically into the two deck mortises, with the teeth upward. Tongues end flush at the bed plane; rail shoulders rest on the deck. Both mounts are clearance seats, not snap fits. Bond with filament-compatible adhesive and allow to cure. Two separated tongues resist twist; retention depends on adhesive.
4. Hold the deck on the desk. Hold the scraper wand by one end and gently hold the wand with its long axis across the rail plane and the leading .3 mm of its 2.4 mm rounded nose overlapping the rear top edge of the teeth; move the nose ALONG the row of teeth (X direction), tilting slightly so only the nose contacts. It is a freely handheld tool, NOT a captive slider. Lift it off to return. Do not press teeth into skin, use as a cutting tool, or apply body weight. Small parts: adult maker model, not for small children.
5. Tooth pitch sets spacing, tooth depth sets peak-to-valley travel, and shape selects flat-topped trapezoid, narrow trapezoid or shallow sawtooth profiles. Every peak has at least 1 mm of flat land; no knife tips. Rail rise, sheet, deck and scraper dimensions customize real solids. There is intentional scraper/rail contact during use; assembled preview places the wand beside the deck, not in a simulated contact pose. No motion, force or sound simulation is claimed.
6. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text and bundled icons use a 20×20 closed heightfield Boolean engraving on the front deck, safely away from mortises. White holes remain unengraved on continuous backing. This is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D reconstruction. STL coordinates require explicit mm confirmation; one closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom ASCII/binary mesh is scaled, rotated, placed and Boolean-subtracted exactly. Concavities, open meshes, overhangs and multiple shells are rejected; no convex-hull replacement or repair.
7. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card , CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Measured sheet/socket and low runner construction inform this original reconstruction. Teeth, paired mounting tongues, deck, wand and interaction are new. No raw reference STL redistributed. All imports stay local; STL stores no colors or unit metadata. Computational mesh/fit checks are not physical print tests.
