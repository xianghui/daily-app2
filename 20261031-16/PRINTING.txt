Grain & Rain — three-part rain cassette kit card.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale in PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Default carrier 140 × 144 mm. The trough is open-side up; lid top-face up; fill plug cap-down. No supports intended. Inspect the small peg/slot fit in your slicer. No physical print, assembly, sound or grain-flow test performed.
2. Snip twelve low runners 1.6 mm outside the part envelopes. Trim nubs, never functional tabs or receiving holes. All three pieces are enclosed in a real connected frame. Source 1.6 mm sheet and 1.700012 mm receiving direction inform sheet + .15 mm total allowance by default (.10 mm was measured in the source). Tongues are redesigned 4.5 mm long. Print one small peg/slot region as a material/printer calibration before the whole cassette; no universal fit is claimed.
3. With the trough empty, lower the lid vertically over all four rectangular tongues. Its underside rests on the perimeter and all full-height baffles. Apply filament-compatible adhesive ONLY along perimeter, peg seats and baffle tops; assemble, clamp gently, wipe stray glue and let cure. These are clearance seats, not snap locks. The underside, baffles and perimeter remain uninterrupted; all personalization is in the external rear landing. Do not try to close the lid with grains in the glue.
4. Through the front square fill opening, add a SMALL pinch of clean dry grains, ideally irregular 1.5–2.5 mm across their narrow direction. Do not pack the chamber; keep below 10% of cavity volume. The 4–6 mm alternating passages route grains around each baffle. Insert the square plug stem down through the opening, cap outside, then bond cap to lid and let cure. The plug is not positively retained without adhesive. Tape the exterior seams if your print leaks. This is not a calibrated sieve, watertight vessel or food-safe container.
5. Slowly invert end-for-end over a tray: dry grains traverse alternating end gaps and strike the chamber for a miniature rain-like sound. Actual flow, sound and duration depend on grains, orientation, friction, layer texture and printer; never guaranteed. Keep away from moisture. Adult craft model only; small parts/grains are a choking risk. No load, pressure or vigorous shaking.
6. Width, length, depth, baffle pitch, passage, sheet and floor thickness change the actual solids. Three rear landing silhouettes and protected text/icon/image/STL engravings personalize the exterior without penetrating the chamber. All units mm; STL stores neither colour nor unit metadata.
7. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG become a bounded 20×20 closed-heightfield Boolean engraving, not full-3D reconstruction from a photo or exact contour tracing. Bright holes remain unengraved on a continuous backing. Convex, flat-footprint ASCII/binary STL becomes an exact Boolean engraving cutter; unsupported concavity, disconnected/open/nonmanifold meshes and overhangs are explicitly rejected, never hulled or repaired. User files stay local; samples are synthetic.
8. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card , CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Only measured sheet/socket and low-breakaway carrier construction informs the reconstruction. Closed rain chamber, alternating baffles, lid keys, fill plug and protected landing are new. No raw third-party mesh bundled. Computational mesh/assembly checks do not constitute physical testing.
