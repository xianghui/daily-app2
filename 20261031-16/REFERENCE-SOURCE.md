Local STL candidates and project instructions were inspected first: Keycap Studio switch template and Weave fabric link are not pierced sheet/card interfaces. Their actual mesh measurements and rejection reasons are in reference-measurements.json. The public kit-card STL was downloaded anew to /tmp/grain-rain-reference.stl, parsed and sectioned before selecting final geometry. Its SHA-256, bounding box, 30,616 triangles, 27 edge-adjacent components and nonwatertight defects are recorded. No source repair or raw mesh redistribution.

Measured source sheet 1.6 mm and receiving direction 1.700012 mm map directly to sheet and sheet+fit. Source receiving length 4.5 mm informed a redesigned 4.5 mm lid tongue; new receiving length is 4.5+fit. Proposed default total allowance .15 mm differs from source .10 mm. Source low runners at .21–.22 mm motivate breakaway bridges; redesigned runners are .4–.6 mm thick for practical layer counts. Carrier, twelve accessible tabs, original baffles, enclosure, four lid keys, fill port/plug and guarded personalization region are reconstructed with CSG, not source mesh re-exports or convex hulls. No endorsement or physical testing claimed.
