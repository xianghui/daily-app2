Rise & Meniscus — three-part parallel-slide capillary jig kit card.
1. Print bed-flat, 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Carrier defaults to 140 × 144 mm. All tongues and guide faces rest on the bed; no supports intended. Computational geometry checks are not a physical print or capillary experiment.
2. Snip twelve runners 1.6 mm outside the part envelopes; trim nubs. Keep the two narrow central separator prongs intact. The plinth, left guide and right guide are distinct after snipping. Calibrate a tongue and slot first: source sheet 1.6 mm and receiving thickness 1.700012 mm motivate sheet + fit. Default .15 mm TOTAL clearance is proposed, not universal. Tongues are 4.5 mm wide, plinth-thickness long.
3. Lower each guide's two tongues straight down into its plinth sockets; shoulders sit on the top surface. Guide planes are 16 mm apart. Use water-resistant filament-compatible adhesive at the four plinth sockets only and let cure. Joints are clearance seats, not snap locks. Guide channels remain open and slides remain removable.
4. Supply TWO matched clean glass slides, nominally 25 × 75 mm, with thickness matching Glass slide thickness (.8–1.4 mm). Smooth-edged acrylic may be tried, but different wetting changes the experiment. Do not force glass. Lower slides one at a time into the paired open-top channels on BOTH guides. Their bottom edges rest 7 mm above the plinth top. Each channel is glass thickness + fit wide. Nominal inner spacing is gap, but loose lateral play permits gap ± fit; the central separator is gap - fit wide (>= .9 mm in supported ranges). Use thin nonabsorbent shims at the OUTER channel walls if repeatability is needed. Do not put glue or shims in the capillary slit.
5. Put the jig in an external broad shallow transparent tray. Add room-temperature water until about 2–3 mm ABOVE the slide bottom edges (plinth top + 9–10 mm). Three plinth drain holes admit water. Observe the liquid between clean slides against a dry background; compare separately printed gap settings under the same conditions. No pump, tubing or sealed chamber. Do not use solvents, heat or hazardous liquids. Adult educational kit; fragile glass and small removable parts.
6. The ideal parallel-plate estimate h = 2*gamma*cos(theta)/(rho*g*gap) gives about 9.8 mm at 1.5 mm for clean completely wetting water/glass (gamma=.072 N/m, rho=1000 kg/m3, g=9.81 m/s2). This is context, not a calibrated reading or simulation: contact angle, gap play, cleanliness and edge effects dominate. Rise may be too small to see. Physical printing, assembly, wetting and rise have NOT been tested. Tick marks are 5 mm spacings above the shoulder, not a water-level zero.
7. Protected rear-plinth text/icon/image/STL engravings cannot reach sockets or slide channels; .4–.6 mm cut depth leaves >=1.4 mm backing. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG become 20 × 20 sampled closed-heightfield Boolean engravings, not full 3D reconstruction or exact tracing. Bright holes stay unengraved on continuous backing. Convex closed flat-footprint ASCII/binary STL is subtracted as its exact transformed mesh. Concave, open, nonmanifold, disconnected, overhanging or excessive sources are rejected, not hulled or repaired. All files stay local. STL has neither colours nor unit metadata.
8. Reference: White Cat Kit Card / Mike Kasberg / https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card , CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Measured pierced-sheet fits and low carrier runners inform the original reconstruction. Paired glass channels, separator prongs, drain plinth, dual guide geometry and capillary application are new. No raw reference STL is bundled.
