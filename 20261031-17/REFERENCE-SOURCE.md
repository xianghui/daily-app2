# Reference source and design decisions

White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg: https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card and https://www.printables.com/model/865769-theft-of-fire-white-cat-kit-card . CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . See REFERENCE-LICENSE.txt.

Local STL files were sought first. Keycap Studio's actual switch-template mesh and Weave's actual diamond-link mesh were parsed and measured, and their READMEs inspected. Neither is a pierced-sheet/carrier assembly. Kit Card Studio's README describes procedural gingerbread parts, not a suitable existing source STL. Local candidate hashes/dimensions and rejection reasons are in reference-measurements.json.

The public STL was downloaded anew to /tmp/rise-meniscus-reference.stl before final geometry selection and parsed with trimesh. It has 30,616 triangles, dimensions 179.820 × 178.020 × 3.190 mm, 27 edge-adjacent components and nonwatertight defects. SHA-256 5cf08e5700cc18ded860a26c8584bb97dff324bd88f54a19f89fef8d87cf73ff. Three actual cross-sections at Z=.1, .5, 1.59 mm are in evidence/reference-sections.png. Units are assumed mm, corroborated by sheet scale; STL contains no unit metadata. No raw third-party STL is redistributed, repaired, or used as a printable import.

Source 1.6 mm sheet and four measured 4.5 × 1.700012 mm slot loops map to guide thickness, 4.5 mm tongue width, and sheet+fit receiving thickness. Default .15 mm total allowance differs deliberately from the measured .10 mm. The other slot direction becomes 4.5+fit; tongue depth is redesigned to the 2–2.6 mm plinth thickness. Low source runners at .21–.22 mm inform removable bridges, redesigned .4–.6 mm thick. These are original Manifold CSG solids, not a source re-export or convex hull.

The drained plinth, matched glass-channel combs, central separator, removable external slides and capillary-gap application are new. Glass slots are glassThickness+fit; nominal slide inner gap is gap, with loose play gap±fit. The central prong is gap-fit, at least .9 mm across supported ranges. Physical printing, glass fit, wetting and capillary performance are untested. Adhesive is required only in four fixed plinth sockets. Printing and material calibration are mandatory before glass assembly.

Read-only visual reference: keycap_generator2 README, styles.css, App.tsx, Viewer.tsx, worker.ts, export.ts, imageTrace.ts and geometry/stl.ts were inspected. Concrete patterns: 374 px left control rail, compact theme header, tactile raised buttons and pressed selections, paired sliders/numbers, numbered collapsible sections, large right viewer, phone preview-first layout, checkerboard conversion, local fonts, worker-owned CSG and current-state export gating. The source tree and old daily_app tree were not edited. Shared import/viewer/persistence/preflight infrastructure was adapted from the author's previous daily-app2 studio; the physical kit builder, assembly poses and independent capillary-specific checks are new.
