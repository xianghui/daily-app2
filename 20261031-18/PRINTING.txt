Hum & Flutter — five-part kazoo-style membrane kit card.
1. Print all parts bed-flat, 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. No supports intended. Carrier defaults to 154 × 176 mm. Computational checks are not physical print or acoustic tests.
2. Snip twenty low runners 1.6 mm OUTSIDE each part envelope, then trim nubs. Keep all eight 4.5 mm wide tongues. Source sheet 1.6 mm and receiving thickness 1.700012 mm motivate sheet+fit. Default .15 mm TOTAL clearance is a proposed calibration starting point, not universal. Test one tongue/slot first.
3. Lower each cheek's two bottom tongues into the four floor sockets; shoulders rest on the floor. Both cheeks face lengthwise and leave BOTH duct ends open. Bond the four fixed seats with filament-compatible adhesive, keeping the inner air path clean. Cure fully.
4. Lower the pierced roof over the four top tongues and onto the cheek shoulders. Bond and seal the roof/cheek and floor/cheek seams from OUTSIDE only; open inlet/outlet ends must remain clear. This is a clearance-seated assembly, not a snap fit or airtight joint without sealant. Geometry checks cover vertical insertion paths and nominal dimensions, not air leakage.
5. Supply a thin 12–18 mm square of clean waxed paper or suitable craft membrane, enough to cover the chosen aperture and clamp. Lay it over the roof opening WITHOUT glue across the vibrating opening. Bond or tape the printed annular clamp on its outside perimeter to sandwich the film. The preview has a labelled estimated .10 mm film allowance; membrane and adhesive are external and not exported. Trim excess only after curing. Avoid over-tensioning; film compliance is experimental.
6. Hold the open duct entrance near the lips and HUM gently through it, leaving the opposite end free. This is a kazoo-style pressure-driven membrane experiment, not a whistle that works by blowing alone. Do not place small parts in the mouth; do not share mouth-contact craft pieces. Printed surfaces are not certified food-safe. Adult craft model. Sound, membrane response, sealing, fit, printing and assembly have NOT been physically tested. Try a different clean film or tension if no buzz occurs; no pitch or loudness promise.
7. Protected rear-floor text/icon/image/STL engravings cannot reach sockets or the duct. Depth .4–.6 mm leaves >=1.4 mm backing. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG use 20 × 20 sampled closed-heightfield Boolean engraving, not full 3D reconstruction or exact outline tracing. Bright holes stay unengraved on continuous backing. Closed convex flat-footprint ASCII/binary STL is subtracted as the exact transformed mesh. Concave, open, nonmanifold, disconnected, overhanging or excessive sources are rejected, not hulled or repaired. All files stay local. STL stores neither colours nor units.
8. Reference: White Cat Kit Card / Mike Kasberg / https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card , CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Measured pierced-sheet interfaces and low runners inform an original reconstruction. The open acoustic duct, double-ended cheeks, perforated roof, film clamp and kazoo application are new. No raw reference mesh is bundled.
