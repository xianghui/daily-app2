# Reference source and reconstruction

White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg: https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card and https://www.printables.com/model/865769-theft-of-fire-white-cat-kit-card . CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The source LICENSE was downloaded; see REFERENCE-LICENSE.txt.

Local STL candidates were sought first under /Volumes/ExternalHD/3d_printing_apps/. Keycap Studio's switch template and Weave's diamond-link were actually parsed, and both READMEs inspected. Their cavities/interlocking links do not provide pierced-sheet/carrier interfaces. Kit Card Studio's README describes procedural house panels, not a suitable existing STL. Local candidate hashes, dimensions and rejection reasons are in reference-measurements.json.

Before selecting final geometry, the public STL was freshly downloaded to /tmp/hum-flutter-reference.stl and parsed with trimesh. SHA-256: 5cf08e5700cc18ded860a26c8584bb97dff324bd88f54a19f89fef8d87cf73ff. Binary STL, 30,616 triangles, dimensions 179.820 × 178.020 × 3.190 mm, 27 edge-adjacent components, nonwatertight defects. STL has no units: mm is assumed and corroborated by the source sheet scale. Actual sections at Z=.1, .5 and 1.59 mm are plotted in evidence/reference-sections.png. The raw mesh is not redistributed, repaired or accepted as a printable import.

Measured sheet planes 0/1.6 mm and four actual 4.5 × 1.700012 mm receiving loops map directly to sheet, fixed 4.5 mm tongue width, and sheet+fit. Default .15 mm TOTAL clearance deliberately differs from source .10 mm. The second socket dimension becomes 4.5+fit; tongue depth is redesigned to match 2–2.6 mm floor/roof thickness. Low source runners at .21–.22 mm inform bridges redesigned to .4–.6 mm thickness, with accessible snips 1.6 mm outside each envelope. All five parts are original Manifold CSG solids, not an unchanged re-export or convex hull.

The open-ended duct, double-ended cheek tongues, two-tier floor/roof sockets, membrane aperture, separate annular clamp, protected rear signature and acoustic application are new. There are eight fixed clearance seats, not snap fits. Adhesive and external seam sealing are required. Thin external film and tape/adhesive are required; the estimated .10 mm film allowance is preview-only. No physical printing, assembly, sealing or sound test was performed. No predicted pitch, loudness or guaranteed buzz is claimed.

Read-only visual reference: keycap_generator2 README.md, src/styles.css, src/App.tsx, src/Viewer.tsx, src/worker.ts, src/export.ts, src/imageTrace.ts and src/geometry/stl.ts were inspected. Applied patterns: 374 px left control rail, sticky compact theme/brand header, tactile raised buttons and pressed states, paired sliders/numeric mm fields, numbered collapsible sections, large right preview, phone preview-first stack, checkerboard conversion, locally bundled Fredoka/Rubik, worker-owned CSG and exact-current-state export gating. The reference tree and old daily_app tree were not edited. Generic studio/import/viewer/persistence/test infrastructure was adapted from the author's earlier daily-app2 studio; the five-part builder, assembly transforms, carrier layout and duct-specific independent checks are new.
