Film & Frame — six-part soap-film dipping cage kit card.
1. Print millimeters at 100%, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three perimeters. All seven exports (card and six clean parts) lie on Z=0. No supports intended. Use individual parts on small beds.
2. Snip TWENTY-FOUR bed-level carrier runners 1.6 mm outside the parts, trim nubs and discard the carrier. Never cut shoulders or functional tongues.
3. Calibrate first with one post and bottom collar. Measured reference sheet 1.6 mm; socket loops 4.5 × 1.700012 mm. Tongue 4.4 × sheet1.6 with .10 TOTAL allowance recreates that cross-section; default .15 and .10–.35 range are proposed printer-dependent allowances, not guaranteed press fits. Deburr elephant foot. Do not force joints.
4. Place the unmarked collar flat on a desk. Stand four posts in its four slots: broad faces parallel to X, thin sheet along Y. Their lower shoulders rest on the collar top; lower tongues end .05 mm above its underside. Dry fit; bond all four lower seats with a water-resistant adhesive suitable for the filament. Cure per adhesive instructions.
5. Lower the signature collar vertically over all four upper tongues. Its bottom face rests on the four upper shoulders; tongues stop .05 mm below its top face. Bond FOUR upper seats and cure. Eight glued joints total: clearance seats are NOT snap locks. Upper collar height is clear post height + collar thickness. No bearings, magnets or metal hardware.
6. Dip the whole open cage into a separate tray of ordinary soap-bubble solution, lift by the outboard signature grip, and observe films spanning different faces. Gently re-dip to reset. A dish, solution and water-resistant adhesive are external consumables, not printed parts. No film surfaces are included in the STL or falsely simulated in preview. Film topology and lifetime are not guaranteed; FDM texture, chemistry and wetting matter. Do not blow into or ingest solution. Rinse and dry; this is a desk teaching craft, not a pressure vessel or children's toy.
7. X/Y spans32–42, clear shoulder-to-shoulder height28–44, collar thickness2.4–3.2, posts1.6–2, tongues4.4–5.2 mm. Square, rounded and chamfered outer-collar silhouettes change geometry while leaving the four functional seats fixed. Inner apertures are span minus8 mm. Minimum socket side wall >2 mm in all allowed profiles; calibrated fit is total allowance, half each side. Carrier200–230 mm square with adjustable spacing and24 accessible .8–1.4 wide,.4–.6 thick tabs.

Text/icons/PNG/JPEG/simple SVG engrave only the outboard top-collar grip, .4–.6 mm depth with ≥1.8 mm backing. 20×20 sampled threshold/grayscale height field is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D photo reconstruction or exact outline tracing. Bright holes remain unengraved. ASCII/binary STL is Boolean-subtracted using exact imported triangles; supported input is one closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid. Concavities, hollow shells and overhangs are rejected, never substituted with a convex hull. Confirm mm, orient, scale/rotate/place. Local bounded payloads restore through named saves and JSON. STL stores no preview colours.
