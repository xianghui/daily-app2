Ink & Wick — three-part paper-chromatography rack kit card.
1. Print flat Z=0 in millimeters at 100%, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three perimeters. No supports intended. Card and three clean parts supplied. Do not scale the finished card to tune the fit.
2. Snip TWELVE bed-level carrier runners 1.6 mm outside the parts; trim nubs and discard carrier. Never clip functional tongues or notch walls.
3. Calibrate one rack tongue and foot first. Source sheet1.6 and socket4.5 ×1.700012 mm. Tongue4.4 × sheet1.6 with .10 TOTAL allowance reconstructs that fit; default .15 and .10–.35 are new printer/material-dependent starting values. Deburr first-layer expansion; do not force.
4. Stand the rack upright with its broad face along X and thickness along Y. Lower both bottom tongues vertically into the two separate feet; shoulders sit on the foot tops, tongues finish .05 mm above the bed. Foot centers are separated by the selected rack post span. Bond BOTH fixed joints with a water-resistant filament-compatible adhesive and cure. These are clearance seats, not snap locks. No bearings, magnets or metal hardware.
5. Place a separate shallow WATER dish beneath the open window, between the feet. Use 6–8 mm wide chromatography/filter-paper strips. Pencil a baseline near one end, add a tiny water-soluble ink dot above the intended water line, fold the dry opposite end over the upper rail at one of THREE registration notches, and optionally secure with a small external paper clip. Notches are 10 mm wide and 2 mm deep, keeping ≥6 mm header backing. They register paper, not clamp it. Adjust paper length so ONLY its bottom edge touches water; the ink spot must stay above water. Upper-rail notch-floor height = hanging height −2 + foot thickness. Lift out and dry strips after observing. The rack has no water reservoir, chemical seal, or guaranteed separation result.
6. Use ordinary washable water-soluble inks and water only. No solvents, flames, heat or food contact. Paper, shallow dish, water, ink, optional clips and adhesive are external consumables, not printed parts. Keep water below the rail and fixed joints; place on a stable surface. Three paper stations are centered at X=−span/4,0,+span/4; station pitch11–15.5 mm. Wider paper may overlap; keep strips separated.
7. Adjustable post span44–62, height38–54, foot depth32–42, foot thickness2.4–3.2, rack sheet1.6–2 mm. Three foot silhouettes preserve sockets. Tongues4.4–5.2 plus total allowance. Rack side webs8 mm; top header8 mm; open lower window. Shoulders overlap the sockets by≥1.225 mm. Foot centers lie under posts; feet extend equally in front/behind for stance. This is a small craft rack, not a load-bearing fixture.
8. Text/icons/PNG/JPEG/simple SVG engrave the OUTBOARD signature-foot grip, .4–.6 mm depth with≥1.8 mm backing. Image conversion is a bounded20×20 threshold/grayscale triangular height field: 2D-to-relief, not full3D reconstruction. Bright holes remain unengraved. ASCII/binary STL uses exact validated triangles as a Boolean cutter; only one closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid is supported. Concavity/open/nonmanifold/overhangs are rejected, never hull-replaced. Confirm mm and select up axis before loading; scale/rotate/place. Assets stay local and round-trip in bounded JSON. STL stores no preview color.
