Wrap & Tuft — four-part open-horseshoe yarn-winding kit card.
1. Print flat Z=0 at 100% in millimeters, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three perimeters. Card and four clean parts supplied. No supports intended; inspect your slicer. All dimensions are mm.
2. Snip SIXTEEN bed-level carrier runners 1.6 mm outside the four parts. Trim nubs and discard the carrier. Do not clip the functional spacer tongues, socket walls, winding rims or the open mouths.
3. Calibrate one spacer tongue and frame socket first. Measured source sheet1.6 and sockets4.5 ×1.700012. Tongue4.4 ×sheet1.6 with .10 TOTAL allowance reconstructs that opening. Default .15 and .10–.35 are proposed printer/material-dependent allowances, not universal press fits. Remove first-layer expansion; do not force.
4. Put the plain frame flat with its mouth pointing away. Stand both shouldered spacers at X=±6 in the two handle sockets; their broad faces run X/Z. Bottom tongues end .05 above the bed and shoulders sit on the frame top. Lower the signature frame, art face up, over both upper tongues, with both mouths and handles aligned. The two frames are separated by the selected6–10 mm free gap. Upper tongues stop .05 below the upper face. Bond FOUR fixed seats with filament-compatible adhesive and cure fully. Clearance seats are NOT snap locks; adhesive is required. No bearings, magnets or metal fasteners.
5. Yarn craft: before winding, lay a separate strong tying strand in the inter-frame gap along the INNER horseshoe curve; keep its ends accessible through the open top mouth. Wind a soft yarn bundle around BOTH frames' nominal8 mm-wide horseshoe rim. Leave the bottom handle sector unwound; work the continuous left, upper and right arcs rather than crossing the open mouth. Thin yarn and light tension are starting points, not a proven capacity. The open inner aperture and mouth must stay clear.
6. With the bundle supported in one hand, an adult can carefully cut the wound loops along the OUTER rim, using the clear inter-frame gap as access. Spacers live entirely on the outboard handle, not in the winding/cutting lane. Tighten and knot the prepositioned tying strand around the cut bundle. Slide the tied bundle toward and out through the open mouth; trim the tuft by hand. Do not cut the printed rims or pull hard on the thin sheet spacers. Yarn, tying strand, scissors and adhesive are external supplies. Keep fingers away from blades. This is a craft former, not a sharp tool or a guaranteed spherical pom-pom process; test a small bundle first.
7. Ring outside span44–62, open mouth14–20, free cutting gap6–10, frame thickness2.4–3.2, spacer sheet1.6–2, tongue4.4–5.2. Square, round and octagonal silhouettes preserve8 mm nominal radial/side bands (octagon facet-normal minimum7.391) and identical outboard seats. Minimum inner opening28 mm; the mouth is open through both frames and unobstructed in the whole gap. Rounded profiles are kinder to yarn than faceted corners. Sand edges before use.
8. Grip text/icons/PNG/JPEG/simple SVG and exact validated STL cutters engrave only the signature handle medallion, .4–.6 deep with≥1.8 backing, away from the sockets. Image conversion is a bounded20×20 threshold/grayscale triangular height field: 2D-to-relief, NOT full3D reconstruction. Bright holes remain unengraved. STL supports a single closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid; open, concave, disconnected, nonmanifold and overhanging meshes are explicitly rejected, never hull-replaced or auto-repaired. Confirm mm, choose up-axis, then scale/rotate/place. All files stay local, portable bounded JSON stores actual payloads. STL has no preview colours.
