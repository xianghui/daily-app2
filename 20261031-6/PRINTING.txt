Drop & Grade — eight-part stacked size-grading kit card.
1. Print flat at 100% in millimeters, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three perimeters. No supports intended; inspect the slicer. The connected card holds all eight assembly parts on 32 bed-level breakaway runners; clean individual parts are also supplied.
2. Snip all THIRTY-TWO runners 1.6 mm outside parts. Trim nubs and discard the carrier. Do not clip the functional shelf tongues, socket walls or gauge rims. The three round tokens are deliberately small: keep away from children and pets; this is an adult teaching model, not a child-safe toy.
3. Reference sheet planes are Z=0/1.6 mm; measured sockets are 4.5 ×1.700012 mm. A 4.4 mm tongue, 1.6 mm shelf and .10 mm TOTAL socket allowance reconstruct these interfaces. Default .15 and range .10–.35 are proposed material/printer-dependent allowances, not universal fits. Print one cheek and shelf to calibrate; remove first-layer expansion and never force the joint.
4. Lay one cheek on its outer face. Insert the three shelves sideways into its indexed sockets: solid signature shelf at the lowest level8 mm, smaller gauge at8+pitch, larger gauge at8+2pitch. All tongues are centered along shelf depth. The signature lug points toward the same open front. Push the other cheek straight onto all three opposing tongues, then stand the frame on its two broad feet. Six shoulders seat against the cheeks; tongues stop .05 mm short of the outer faces. Bond SIX fixed tongue/socket seats with filament-compatible adhesive and cure. These are clearance joints, NOT snap locks. No bearings, magnets or metal fasteners.
5. Place a token FLAT and centered over the upper hole, release gently, and observe where it is caught. Largest diameter=small+8 stays at the upper gauge. Medium diameter=small+4 passes the upper hole and rests on the middle gauge. Smallest passes both holes and lands on the solid lower shelf. Upper bore=medium+selected gauge allowance; middle bore=small+allowance. Diametral passing play .6–1.2 mm and caught radial overhang1.4–1.7 mm are geometry calculations, NOT a guarantee of dynamic sorting: tilt, edge catches, bounce, friction and printer error matter. Guide/center by hand, and retrieve through the open front/back. This is a discrete flat-token classification demonstration, not a calibrated coin sorter or high-speed hopper.
6. Keep the shelves horizontal. The preview shows ideal final catch positions for the THREE supplied flat tokens, not an animation or simulation. Exploded view separates the cheeks horizontally and lifts tokens for explanation. No positive token retention is intended; tokens remain removable. No external consumables except the fixed-joint adhesive. Do not feed arbitrary small objects.
7. Shelf span40–48, depth32–38, pitch16–22, small token10–14, gauge allowance .6–1.2, cheek thickness2.4–3.2, shelf/token sheet1.6–2, tongue4.4–5.2 mm. Square, rounded and clipped-corner shelf profiles preserve hole walls and the same six joints. Card230–260 ×230–260 mm; use individual parts on smaller beds.
8. Text/icons/PNG/JPEG/simple SVG and exact validated STL cutters engrave the lower shelf's protected outboard signature lug, .4–.6 mm deep, leaving at least1.0 mm backing. They never modify the token drop lane, gauge bores or sockets. Image conversion is a bounded20×20 threshold/grayscale triangular height field: 2D-to-relief, NOT full3D reconstruction. Bright holes remain unengraved. STL accepts one closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid; concave, open, disconnected, nonmanifold and overhanging inputs are explicitly rejected, never hull-replaced or auto-repaired. Confirm mm, choose up-axis, scale, rotate and place. Files stay local; bounded JSON stores real payloads. STL has no preview colours.
