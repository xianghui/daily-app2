Level & Loop — three-part communicating-column U-tube stand kit card.
1. Print the connected card flat at 100% millimeters in PLA/PETG, 0.16–0.20 mm layers and three perimeters. No supports intended. Inspect slicer layers. Card 210–240 ×180–210 mm; clean individual parts work on smaller beds.
2. Snip TWELVE accessible bed-level runners 1.6 mm outside parts; trim nubs, discard carrier and keep the base plus two towers. Do not cut the central tower tongues or cord eyes. Adult maker apparatus; small parts and water, not a children's toy.
3. Print one tower and base as a calibration pair. Source measured sheet1.6 and socket4.5×1.700012 mm: tongue4.4 and total gap.10 reconstruct that section. Default gap.15 (range.10–.35) is proposed printer/material allowance, not universal. Remove elephant foot. Insert each tongue vertically through its base socket; shoulders rest on top and tongue tips stop.05 above the bed. Bond TWO fixed seats with filament-compatible adhesive and cure. No snap retention or load rating.
4. Supply ONE transparent silicone tube OD4–6 mm (chosen in editor), approximately 2×(towerHeight+4−span/2)+PI×span/2+40 mm long; ends stay open at equal ambient pressure. This is NOT a pressure/vacuum apparatus or a medical/food-contact device. Use a tube with bore at least3 mm to reduce capillary effects; select a compatible wall/OD. Supply FOUR soft cord loops about80 mm each (trim to tube/knots). Adhesive is the only other consumable. No bearings, metal fasteners or magnets.
5. Form a smooth U with centerline bend radius span/2, 18–25 mm (at least3×chosenOD in supported ranges). Tube-dependent minimum bend radius must be checked against supplier advice; if it kinks, increase span or use thinner/smaller softer tube. Position the U bottom6 mm above the base top, along the FRONT of both towers. Tie gently through paired1.25 mm-radius cord eyes at tower heights32 and height−8 mm, around the tube. Never pinch the bore closed; cord provides adjustable restraint, not a modeled interference clamp.
6. Add a small amount of room-temperature colored water with a dropper, keeping both upper ends open. Tap gently to free bubbles. With the stand level and one continuous liquid, compare the two menisci at rest: equal atmospheric pressure predicts equal elevation, not necessarily identical meniscus shape. Drain and dry after use; keep away from electronics. No pressure sources, hot liquids or hazardous fluids. A tray beneath is recommended. The5 mm engraved ticks are nominal geometry guides, not a calibrated scale.
