Climb & Grip — four-part cord-climber kit card.
1. Print the connected card flat at 100% mm, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three perimeters. No supports intended. Small parts; adult craft prototype, not load-bearing.
2. Snip SIXTEEN accessible runners 1.6 mm outside the parts, trim nubs and discard the carrier. Keep crossarm, mascot body and two pull toggles. Protect tongues and cord eyes.
3. Calibrate the body tongue and arm slot first: measured source sheet1.6 and socket4.5 by1.700012 mm are reconstructed by tongue4.4, sheet1.6 and total allowance.10. Default.15 is proposed, not universal. With arm horizontal, insert the mascot tongue UP from below until shoulders touch the arm underside. Tip stops.05 below the arm top. Bond this one fixed joint with filament-compatible adhesive and cure. Mascot hangs below the crossarm.
4. Supply TWO soft braided cords OD1.5–2.5 mm, each about700 mm, a sturdy external horizontal anchor and adhesive. Tie one end of each cord to the anchor at the chosen eye spacing. Pass the free ends down through the two crossarm bores. Pass each through its separate toggle eye and tie a stopper knot LARGER than that eye below it. Verify knots cannot pull through. There is no printed top anchor. Keep all cord away from necks, pets and children.
5. Alternate gentle short pulls on the toggles while allowing the mascot to tilt. Straight cords have clearance; tilted cord envelopes contact opposite bore rims. Contact angle is computed from d/cos(theta)+H*tan(theta)=bore. Rim contact is intentional, NOT a verified friction coefficient, self-lock or climbing-performance claim. Friction, cord stretch and print texture may prevent climbing; try a calibration crossarm with the actual cord first. Do not lubricate and do not apply high loads.
6. Assembled/exploded views show estimated straight cord paths and toggles, not a dynamic simulation. Print exports are always bed-flat parts or the connected card, never assembled overlaps. No metal fasteners, magnets or bearings; cord, external anchor and one adhesive joint are required. No hardware-free motion claim.
7. Personalization engraves only the outboard crossarm lug. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG use a20 by20 sampled threshold or grayscale heightfield, 2D-to-relief not full3D photo reconstruction. ASCII/binary STL uses exact validated convex flat-bottom mesh triangles, never a convex hull replacement. Open/nonmanifold/concave/overhanging inputs are unsupported. Engraving.4–.6 leaves at least1.8 backing. All files stay local, portable JSON includes bounded payloads; STL stores no color or units.
8. Source White Cat Kit Card / Printables865769, Mike Kasberg, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card , CC BY4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Original parametric reconstruction uses measured sheet/socket and low-runner principles, not the source mesh or spacecraft. Raw source STL not redistributed.
9. Computational mesh, fit, insertion, snip and cord-envelope checks are not physical tests. Physical printing, assembly, knot retention, friction, durability and climbing performance have NOT been tested. Calibrate using actual printer/material/cord; no universal tolerance or movement guarantee.
