# Slit & Spectrum

An original **four-part flat-pack transmission-diffraction viewing kit**, not a prism, beam splitter, game or general mesh editor. Snip sixteen real runners, stand the slit and grating walls in the base, then lower the slotted roof over their upper tongues. Add transmission-grating film, a narrow foil slit and an opaque paper wrap. The kit explores colours beside the direct slit image when aimed at diffuse cool white LED light. **Never view the Sun, lasers or intense lamps.** No physical printing, assembly, spectral resolution or optical performance is claimed tested.

## Dimensions and manufacturing

All lengths millimeters. Card120–136 ×176–200; bench76–84; width24–28; wall height22–26; base/roof2–2.6; sheet1.6–2; fit .1–.3 TOTAL; spacing3–4; tabs .8–1.4 wide ×.4–.6 thick; grating window6–10; backing slit1–2 wide. Windows are wallHeight−10 high and share an axis at baseThickness+wallHeight/2. All ranges are validated; unsupported packing/errors block export. Three real presets and three base silhouettes; bounded variations, undo/redo, six local named saves and portable JSON with bounded source payloads.

1. Print opaque PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls, at100% mm scale. No supports intended. Trial-print a wall and base first; calibrate the proposed allowance to your printer/material. Adhesive is required, not a universal press/snap fit.
2. Snip16 runners >=1.6mm outside part edges, trim nubs, preserve tongues/windows. Adult supervision for cutting and small parts.
3. Lower walls into base; lower roof over top tongues. Calibrate then bond4 fixed seats and cure. No hardware or moving retention interface.
4. Tape external500–1000 lines/mm transmission grating over wide window, lines vertical. Tape two straight foil edges over narrow opening to make an approximately .1–.3mm vertical slit. The printed backing aperture alone is too wide for a useful precision spectrum.
5. Wrap the two open sides in opaque paper and tape light leaks/roof slots. External film, foil, paper, tape and adhesive are required; not exported parts. Look through grating toward the slit and diffuse white light, then slightly beside the direct image for the first-order spectrum. No Sun, lasers or intense sources. No wavelength calibration, light-tightness or resolution claim. Preview shows printed geometry, not external materials or light simulation.

## Local imports that change real geometry

File picker works on phones/desktops; drop on the import area where supported. Bundled synthetic fixtures: PNG, JPEG, SVG ring, ASCII wedge and binary convex gem; no personal files included. Replace/remove/reset return to built-ins. Imports engrave the actual isolated base extension by Manifold difference, remote from optical corridor and four sockets.

**PNG/JPEG:** inspect magic bytes and header dimensions before decoding;2MB,<=12MP,<=6000px per side. Normalize <=500px for portable storage; fit/cover crop and shift±25%, light/dark background and threshold1–254. Convert to20×20 luminance samples: threshold mode forms a piecewise-linear silhouette heightfield; grayscale mode maps darkness continuously. A closed indexed heightfield becomes a Boolean engraving cutter .4–.6mm deep. Bright holes remain unengraved, backing>=1.4mm. Checkerboard shows the actual sampled cutout, source beside it. This is **2D-to-relief**, not reconstructing a full object from one photo, and not exact traced vector outlines. Simple SVG paths/shapes (<=2000 elements, no script/resources/effects/text) rasterize locally to256px and follow this method. Text up to8characters and six searchable bundled marks use the same real engraved geometry.

**STL:** binary or complete ASCII,2MB,<=1500triangles, each oriented dimension .2–250mm. Strict weld1e-5mm, incidence/winding, connected positive volume, degeneracy, intersections and bounded convex flat-bottom contract. Open, nonmanifold, hollow, concave, disconnected, touching or unsupported overhanging meshes are rejected with actionable errors. **No convex-hull substitution:** only a bottom-footprint hull for validation, never as output. Select Z/Y/−Z up; changing axis requests reattachment. Confirm millimeter units explicitly; preview source dimensions and oriented mesh. Proportional XY footprint4–7mm, independent Z engraving .4–.6mm, rotation±180°, offsets X±1.5/Y±2. Actual transformed mesh is the Boolean cutter, not unchanged re-export or overlapping shells. Circular6.5mm keepout and minimum1.2mm XY extent validation protect walls/features. Scale factors displayed. Geometry outside keepout blocks export.

Everything stays in browser memory/localStorage. No user file upload or telemetry. JSON includes bounded image pixels/normalized PNG or parsed mesh; restore revalidates. Missing image payload explicitly requests reattachment rather than pretending success.
