Plate & Node — central-support resonance-plate experiment kit card.
1. Print the connected card OR the three clean parts flat at Z=0, 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls, no supports intended. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm. Do not print card AND the same clean parts unless duplicates are wanted.
2. Snip twelve accessible bed-level runners 1.6 mm outside part edges. Trim remaining nubs from the outer edges; do not cut the three tongues or alter socket walls. Carrier is waste. Small parts and sharp cutters: adult craft kit, not a child toy.
3. Trial-print base and pedestal first as a full-size fit coupon. Source measured: 1.6 mm sheet, 4.50 × 1.700012 mm sockets. Retained socket length 4.50; new slot width is sheet + .10–.30 TOTAL allowance, tongue length 4.50 minus allowance. Default .15 is proposed, not universal. Calibrate for material, first-layer expansion and printer before printing the full card.
4. Stand the tapered pedestal vertically. Insert both bottom tongues straight down into the two base sockets; shoulders stop at the base top and tips finish at the underside. Lower the resonance plate horizontally over its central top tongue. The central shoulder supports it, and the tongue ends flush at the top. Glue all three seats with compatible adhesive and let fully cure. No snap-lock, captive motion or universal friction-fit claim.
5. Hold the base on a desk and lightly tap different rim locations with a pencil or fingernail. Compare square, circular and octagonal plates, spans, support width and sheet thickness. Do not push through large bends or apply body weight. Larger/thinner free spans tend to be more compliant, but no spring force, frequency, sound quality or fatigue life is predicted. Use the assembly lift slider only to understand insertion; the cured plate is fixed, not a moving joint.
6. Optional educational extension: a small amount of fine dry sand and an external violin bow or low-power vibration exciter may reveal nodal patterns when resonance is sustained. Bow/exciter and sand are NOT included or generated. Polymer damping, FDM layers, clamping, bonding and excitation strongly affect results; visible patterns are not guaranteed. Hardware-free alternative: gently tap and listen/feel without sand. No audio service, fake nodal animation, simulated modes or browser game.
7. Personalize the front of the fixed base using local PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text or six bundled icons. A 20×20 sampled height-field cutter engraves .4–.6 mm, leaving at least 1 mm backing. Bright holes preserve backing, not through-holes. Threshold/grayscale, fit/crop, light/dark background, footprint 4–7 mm, rotation ±180°, X±1.5/Y±2 mm; radial keep-in 6.5 mm protects all joints. This is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D photo reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Raised art is intentionally unavailable to retain bed-flat sheet geometry and keep the vibrating plate unchanged.
8. STL: ASCII/binary, ≤2 MB, ≤1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per source axis. One closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Explicit millimeter confirmation: STL has no unit metadata. Select up axis before attaching. Proportional XY footprint and independent depth, rotation/placement alter the EXACT Boolean cutter. Concave/hollow/open/nonmanifold/multibody/overhang meshes reject explicitly; no convex-hull substitution or automatic repair. Files stay local; portable JSON includes bounded normalized image/mesh payloads.
