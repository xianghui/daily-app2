Loop & Lather — paired soap-film loop kit card.
1. Print ONE connected card OR its three clean parts flat at Z=0, 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls; no supports intended. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm. Do not print the card AND duplicate parts unless wanted. Adult craft kit; small parts and sharp cutters.
2. Snip twelve accessible bed-level runners 1.6 mm outside part edges. Trim remaining nubs from outer edges. Do not cut the two docking tongues or socket walls. Carrier is waste.
3. Trial-print dock and one loop as a full-size calibration coupon. Source measured: 1.6 mm sheet, 4.50 × 1.700012 mm sockets. Retain 4.50 socket length; slot width = sheet + .10–.30 TOTAL allowance; tongue length = 4.50 minus allowance. Default .15 is proposed, not universal. Calibrate printer/material and first-layer expansion. If too tight, increase allowance or deburr lightly; never force thin loops.
4. Stand each loop upright and lower its tongue into one dock socket. Shoulder stops at the top of the base; tongue tip finishes at the underside. Loose fit and wobble are intentional: NO glue, snap lock or captive motion. Dock spacing measures between loop midplanes; face clearance = spacing minus sheet. Lift each loop straight up at least the sheet thickness to remove. Park indoors on a level dry surface; dock is not a tray or liquid container.
5. External supplies: commercially prepared bubble solution and a shallow tray larger than the loops. Hold both handles, lift out of dock, dip both apertures and bring wetted loops close together without forcing them through one another. Slowly pull them apart by hand to explore stretching films. Try circles, squares and octagons, aperture span 24–40 mm and band 2.4–4 mm. No simulated film, guaranteed catenoid, bubble yield, lifetime or surface-tension measurement is claimed. Do not blow through a mouthpiece; none is generated. Avoid eyes and ingestion; follow solution instructions. Rinse lightly and dry; FDM grooves can retain liquid. Not food-safe or a bath toy. Plain loops need wetting; no hidden cord or hardware required. Physical wetting and film behavior untested.
6. The seated separation/lift slider is an insertion explanation only; it does not simulate film. Assembled, exploded, isolated, de-tabbed and connected-card views share exact printable solids. Exports are always bed-flat, not interpenetrating assembled shells.
7. Personalize the front of the fixed dock using local PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text or six bundled icons. A 20×20 sampled height-field cutter engraves .4–.6 mm, leaving at least 1 mm backing. Bright holes preserve backing, not through-holes. Threshold/grayscale, fit/crop, light/dark background, footprint 4–7 mm, rotation ±180°, X±1.5/Y±2 mm; radial keep-in 6.5 mm protects docking sockets. 2D-to-relief, not full 3D photo reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Raised art is intentionally unsupported to retain bed-flat sheet geometry.
8. STL: ASCII/binary, ≤2 MB, ≤1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per source axis. One closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Explicit millimeter confirmation: STL has no units. Select up axis before attaching. Proportional XY footprint and independent depth, rotation/placement alter the EXACT Boolean cutter. Concave/hollow/open/nonmanifold/multibody/overhang meshes reject explicitly; no hull substitution or automatic repair. All files stay local; portable JSON includes bounded normalized image/mesh payloads.
9. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Actual mesh parsed and sectioned in this run. Measured 1.6 sheets and 4.50 × 1.700012 sockets map to parameters. Proposed .15 allowance differs from source ~.100012. Original handled rings, dock and .4–.6 mm runners; no source triangles/artwork bundled. Source defects are not copied or claimed repaired.
10. Computational closed-mesh, support, release, aperture and insertion checks are NOT physical tests. No physical print, assembly, film, wetting, stability, durability or material test performed. STL stores geometry, not preview colour or units.
