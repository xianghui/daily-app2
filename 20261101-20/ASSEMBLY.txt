Contour & Summit — shoulder-stacked terrain kit card.
1. Print ONE connected card OR its six clean parts, flat at Z=0, 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls, no supports intended. Sheets 1.6–2.4 mm. Do not print the card AND duplicate parts unless wanted. Adult craft kit; small parts and sharp cutters.
2. Snip 24 accessible bed-level runners 1.6 mm outside the part edges. Trim nubs; do not cut slot walls, spine shoulders or the base tongue. Carrier is waste. Parts are named plinth, spine and contours 1–4 from largest to smallest.
3. Trial-print the plinth, spine and top contour as calibration parts. Source mesh measured 1.6 mm sheet and 4.50 × 1.700012 mm sockets. Plinth retains the measured 4.50 length. All slot widths = sheet + .10–.30 TOTAL allowance, not per side; plinth tongue length = 4.50 minus allowance. Default .15 is proposed, not universal. Calibrate printer/material and first-layer expansion. Increase allowance if tight; do not force or bend thin sheets.
4. Lower the spine's short bottom tongue into the plinth socket. Its wide lower shoulders sit on the plinth top and tongue tip reaches the underside. Slot fit is loose; glue is required for a rigid display. Keep the spine upright while the glue cures. Base stability is for a small indoor display, not a load-rated shelf.
5. From above, lower contour 1 (largest) over the top of the spine to its lowest shoulder. Then contours 2, 3 and 4. Slot lengths equal the corresponding upper-step width plus allowance; every higher step is narrower so the insertion path is clear. The narrower upper step passes through the plate; the wider lower step supports it. Elevation interval 6–12 mm, shoulder overlap at least 1 mm per side; clear air gap = interval minus sheet thickness. Glue each slot only after dry fitting, square the plates and cure level. No snap-lock or positive retention claimed. Required external supply: plastic-compatible adhesive; follow its instructions.
6. Silhouettes: circular hill, square mesa and octagonal hill. Contour spans are 100%, 80%, 60% and 40% of the 40–56 mm lower span. Spine widths are 70%, 55%, 40%, 25% and 4.50 mm at successive levels. Four equal elevation intervals explain abstract contour spacing; no geographic dataset or calculated slope accuracy is implied. The insertion-lift control is an explanation only; a glued display does not move. Assembled/exploded/isolated/de-tabbed/card views share exact printable meshes. Exports always use bed-flat orientation, never overlapped assembly shells.
7. Local PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text or six bundled icons engrave the protected front of the fixed plinth, away from the spine socket. A 20×20 sampled height-field cutter removes .4–.6 mm, leaving at least 1 mm backing. Bright holes preserve backing, not through-holes. Threshold/grayscale, fit/crop, light/dark background, footprint 4–7 mm, rotation ±180°, X±1.5/Y±2 mm; radial keep-in 6.5 mm. 2D-to-relief, not full 3D photo reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Raised art is intentionally unsupported to preserve flat-sheet thickness.
8. STL: ASCII/binary, ≤2 MB, ≤1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per source axis. One closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Explicit millimeter confirmation: STL has no units. Select up axis before attaching. Proportional XY footprint and independent depth, rotation/placement alter the EXACT Boolean engraving cutter. Concave/hollow/open/nonmanifold/multibody/overhang meshes reject explicitly; no hull substitution or automatic repair. All files stay local; portable JSON includes bounded normalized image/mesh payloads. Replace/remove/reset are available.
9. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Actual STL parsed and sectioned for this run. Measured sheets and sockets map to generator parameters; wider step slots, shoulder profile and .4–.6 mm runners are proposed redesigns. Source has mesh defects, not copied or claimed repaired. No source mesh/artwork bundled.
10. Computational closed-mesh, support-footprint, shoulder, ordered insertion, collision and runner-release checks are NOT physical tests. No physical print, assembly, strength, stability or material test performed. STL stores geometry, not preview colour or unit metadata.
