Curve & Tempo — cycloidal-cheek pendulum experiment kit card.
1. Print ONE connected card OR four clean parts, flat at Z=0, 100% mm scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls, no supports intended. Sheets 1.6–2.4 mm. Adult craft kit; small parts, cutters and loose thread.
2. Snip 16 accessible bed-level runners 1.6 mm outside part edges. Deburr away from tongues, sockets, cord eyes and cheek curves. Carrier is waste. Keep plinth, fork, bob and length gauge.
3. Source measured 1.6 mm sheet and 4.50 × 1.700012 mm sockets. Plinth keeps measured 4.50 length; socket width = sheet + .10–.30 TOTAL allowance, not per side. Tongue length = 4.50 minus allowance. Default .15 is proposed, not universal; trial-print plinth and fork, calibrate first-layer expansion and material. Never force thin parts.
4. Lower both fork tongues straight into the two sockets together. Broad shoulders stop on plinth top; tongue tips end flush at the underside. Glue both loose-fit joints, square fork upright and cure. Required external plastic-compatible adhesive. No captive movement or load rating claimed.
5. Thread .3–.5 mm soft flexible cord through the 1.6 mm top eye; tie securely and route down the FRONT face to the cusp, never through solid sheet. The finite front rim is an experimental cord guide and lateral tracking is unverified. Use the gauge: the two side notches are exactly 4a apart (28–40 mm). Tie the bob through its upper eye with nominal 4a free cord measured from the cheek cusp to bob eye at rest; actual suspension eye is 4 mm above cusp, so begin with eye-to-eye length 4a+4 mm and adjust knots. Knots and finite eyes change this value. Gently hand-swing in the fork plane, below ~15 degrees first; if cord binds, rides off the edge or bob strikes the fork/base, stop and rethread. Sand cheek edges smooth. No magnets, bearing or metal fastener required. A small loose cord is not a toy for children.
6. Cheeks use x=±[.6+a(t−sin t)], drop=a(1−cos t), 0≤t≤pi, 64 segments per side. a=7–10 mm, fork 64–80 mm, bob radius 5–7 mm. Central 1.2 mm channel and anchor offset intentionally avoid a zero-width sharp cusp; therefore this is an EXPERIMENTAL geometric guide, NOT an accurate isochronous clock or validated tautochrone. String contact/friction, finite thickness, cord stretch, knots and bob mass are not simulated. No frequency or period guarantee. Fork/bob support-free sheet profiles share the selected rounded/square/chamfer style. The separate gauge is removable and parked to the side in assembly view.
7. Assembled view shows the actual printable parts at rest plus a dashed explanatory thread. Exploded view explains foot insertion and thread tie; isolated/de-tabbed/card views share exact meshes. No fake physics animation. Exports are always bed-flat. Add your own .3–.5 mm cord and adhesive; printable kit does not include them.
8. Local PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text or six bundled icons engrave the protected front of fixed plinth, away from both foot sockets. A 20×20 sampled height-field cutter removes .4–.6 mm leaving at least 1 mm backing. Bright holes preserve backing, not through-holes. Threshold/grayscale, fit/crop, dark/light background, footprint 4–7 mm, rotation ±180°, X±1.5/Y±2 mm; radial keep-in 6.5 mm. 2D-to-relief, not 3D photo reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Raised art unsupported to preserve card Z envelope.
9. STL: ASCII/binary ≤2 MB, ≤1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per source axis. One closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid only; concavities/hollows/open/nonmanifold/multibody/overhang inputs explicitly rejected. No hull replacement or repair. Explicit mm confirmation, up-axis before attach, proportional XY footprint and independent engraving depth, rotation and XY placement. Exact accepted source mesh is Boolean-subtracted in fixed base, never near moving cord. Files remain local. Bounded normalized payloads restored in JSON/local designs; no user files committed.
10. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Actual STL parsed and sectioned this run; sheets/socket interfaces measured. Source mesh has defects, used only for design reference. All solids reconstructed; no source mesh/artwork redistributed. Proposed runners .4–.6 mm replace measured .21–.22 mm, and this four-part pendulum assembly is original.
11. Computational manifold, card envelope, support footprint, socket clearances, insertion and snip-release checks are NOT physical printing or dynamic simulation. Physical print, assembly, strength, cord tracking, oscillation, stability and timing remain untested. STL stores neither colours nor units. Put the kit on a stable level table; do not hang loads from it.
