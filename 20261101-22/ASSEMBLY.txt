Steiner & Knot — three-weight thread-junction experiment kit card.
1. Print ONE connected card OR four clean parts, flat at Z=0, 100% mm scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls, no supports intended. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm. Adult craft experiment: small weights, loose cord and cutters.
2. Snip 16 bed-level runners 1.6 mm outside part edges. Deburr away from six tongues, sockets, open cord notches and three drop windows. Keep stage plus north/west/east saddles; carrier is waste.
3. Source measured 1.6 mm sheet and 4.50 × 1.700012 mm sockets. Six reconstructed sockets retain 4.50 mm length; width = sheet + .10–.30 TOTAL allowance (not per side). Tongue length = 4.50 minus allowance. Default .15 is proposed, not universal; trial-print stage and one saddle first, calibrate first-layer expansion/material. Never force thin sheets.
4. Lower both feet of each saddle into its paired sockets at one vertex. Shoulders stop on stage, tongue tips end flush underneath. Glue all six joints, square and cure. Identical saddles are interchangeable; north is the extended vertex. Open notch faces UP; the large drop window is OUTSIDE each saddle, not between the feet.
5. Put the stage on a stable non-slip external riser at least 60 mm tall and no wider than 20 mm across, centered under the solid center. Secure stage and riser with removable tape and protect the table. This external riser is NOT included. Make sure the three drop windows are completely unobstructed and weights cannot hit the table. Use only very light equal 1–3 g craft weights, not heavy loads. This is not a rated suspension structure.
6. Tie three .3–.5 mm soft cords to one small central junction (or join into a compact knot). Lay each branch into an open U notch, carry it over the OUTWARD rim, down through the associated 6 × 8 mm stage window and attach an equal weight below. Leave room for weights to move freely above the table. Smooth all cord contact edges. Rims are NOT pulleys: friction, cord stiffness and knots can cause sticking. Gently nudge the knot; never pull hard or drop weights. No metal bearings, magnets or fasteners are needed; external cord, three equal light weights, narrow riser, tape and plastic-compatible adhesive ARE required.
7. All three guide centers lie at one height. Guide radius 22–28 mm; north apex shifts sideways by 0–8 mm; saddle height 32–44 mm. With ideal equal tensions and a point junction, the stationary point has three 120° branches and minimizes total branch length. A Weiszfeld geometric median is drawn only as a dashed ideal routing guide; it is NOT a simulation or measured equilibrium. Finite rims, knot size, friction and weight swing change behavior. The triangle remains acute in supported ranges. Do not claim the actual knot is a precision Fermat/Steiner construction.
8. Assembled/exploded/de-tabbed/card/isolated views share the exact solids. Dashed cords show ideal approximate routing and are NOT exported; weights and riser are external supplies. Exports are always printable bed-flat, not overlapping assembled shells. Three drop windows and six joints are real Boolean cutouts.
9. Local PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text and six bundled icons engrave the protected center of the fixed stage, away from joints and drop windows. Actual 20×20 sampled threshold/grayscale height-field cutter; .4–.6 mm depth leaves at least 1 mm backing. Bright holes preserve uncut surface, not through-holes. Fit/crop, background, scale 4–7 mm, rotation and XY placement alter geometry. 2D-to-relief, not 3D photo reconstruction or exact tracing. Raised art unsupported to preserve card thickness.
10. STL ASCII/binary ≤2 MB, ≤1500 triangles, .2–250 mm/source axis. One closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Unsupported concavity/hollow/open/nonmanifold/multibody/overhang rejected, never hulled or silently repaired. Confirm mm; select up axis before attach; proportional XY footprint, independent Z depth, rotation and XY placement. Exact mesh is Boolean-subtracted in center stage. All files stay local; bounded portable payloads travel in JSON/local named designs.
11. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Actual mesh parsed/sectioned in this run. Source defects are not inherited; original CSG reconstruction retains measured sheet/socket interfaces. Runners .4–.6 mm replace measured .21–.22 mm. No source mesh or fan-art redistributed.
12. Computational mesh, collision, insertion, cord path, drop-window, runner-release and ideal vector checks are NOT physical printing or material/force simulation. Physical print, assembly, adhesion, stability, cord sliding, accuracy and equilibrium are untested. STL stores neither colours nor units. Stop if any part bends, binds or tips.
