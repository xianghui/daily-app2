Wrap & Resist — cross-lapped cord-friction kit card.
1. Print ONE connected card OR four clean parts at Z=0, 100% mm. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls; no supports intended. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm. Four parts: marked plinth, top-slot fin, bottom-slot fin, shuttle.
2. Snip 16 bed-level runners 1.6 mm outside part edges. Deburr feet, cross slots, cord notches and shuttle. Carrier is waste. Do not nick functional joints.
3. Reference measured 1.6 mm sheet and 4.50 × 1.700012 mm sockets. Four new sockets retain 4.50 length; width = sheet + .10–.30 TOTAL allowance, not per side. Tongue length = 4.50 minus allowance. Trial-print base and fins; calibrate first-layer expansion, material and fit. These are not universal tolerances.
4. Seat TOP-SLOT fin first in the two X-axis base sockets. Hold upright. Lower BOTTOM-SLOT fin vertically across it into the two Y-axis sockets; bottom slot passes over the first fin's upper web. The complementary half-laps have the selected total allowance and stop without bending. Feet end flush below the base, shoulders rest on it. Dry-fit before applying plastic-compatible adhesive to all FOUR feet, square and cure. No forced snap or positive locking is claimed.
5. Secure the base to a non-slip table with removable tape. Use soft .3–.8 mm craft cord. Thread/lay it around the four OUTER fin tips at one of the three matching groove heights. One complete wrap turns around four corners, not a round cylinder. Start with a half wrap; compare gentle hand-drag with one full wrap. Feed cord using the separate shuttle. The two base holes are optional tail-tie points, not bearings. Keep fingers away from rubbing contact, smooth contact edges, and stop if cord snags or fins bend. Do not suspend weights, use elastic cord or rely on it to secure loads.
6. This is a four-corner friction exploration, NOT a calibrated capstan, brake, winch or rope-holding tool. Cord bending, surface roughness, groove shape and material govern drag; no exponential force ratio, friction coefficient, load rating, retention or physical performance claim. The post has four separated webs, not a solid cylinder. External soft cord, removable tape and adhesive are required; no hardware required. The shuttle remains separate and removable.
7. Post half-span 22–28 mm, height 32–44 mm, lane pitch 4–8 mm. Three real rounded edge notches, 2.8 mm diameter with .9 mm penetration, on each of four tips. Matching height lanes: .55×post height minus pitch / center / plus pitch. Assembled/exploded/card/loose/isolated views use same exact solids. Dashed loop is an estimated external cord route only, not exported or a friction simulation. Always export bed-flat parts or connected carrier, never intersecting assembled shells.
8. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text and six bundled icons engrave the protected FRONT plinth patch, away from all four sockets and two eyes. Actual 20×20 sampled threshold/grayscale height-field cutter; .4–.6 mm depth leaves ≥1 mm backing. Bright holes preserve full backing, not through-holes. Fit/crop, background, scale 4–7 mm, rotation and XY placement change the cutter. 2D-to-relief, not 3D photo reconstruction or exact tracing. Raised art is intentionally unsupported for flat sheet thickness.
9. ASCII/binary STL ≤2 MB, ≤1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per source axis. Accepts one closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Unsupported concavity/hollow/open/nonmanifold/multibody/overhang rejected, NEVER hulled or silently repaired. Confirm mm; choose up axis before attaching; proportional XY footprint, independent Z depth, rotation and XY placement. Actual mesh Boolean-subtracted in the protected front patch. All files stay local. Portable JSON and named designs contain bounded normalized assets.
10. Actual reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Downloaded STL parsed and sectioned during this run. Source defects not inherited; new original CSG reconstruction adapts measured sheet/socket interfaces and carrier idea. Proposed .4–.6 mm runners replace source .21–.22 mm. No raw source STL or fan-art redistributed.
11. Computational closed-manifold, per-component winding/volume, socket/half-lap insertion, separation, groove/route, floor, card and runner checks are NOT physical testing. Physical printing, assembly, adhesion, cord sliding, durability and friction are UNTESTED. STL has no colours or units.
