Tow & Trace — straight-guide pursuit-curve drawing kit.
1. Print ONE connected card OR six separate clean parts, all Z=0. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls, 100% mm. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm; shoe axle rises to 4×sheet+.6 mm. No supports intended. Card width230–260, height200–240 mm. STL contains no colours or unit metadata.
2. Snip 24 runners 1.6 mm outside each part and deburr. Never nick the guide slot, tongues, shaft or bores. Carrier is waste. Parts: guide, two feet, tow shoe with integral axle, pencil arm, keeper.
3. Reference sheet measured 1.6 mm and sockets4.50×1.700012 mm. Four new foot sockets preserve4.50 length, width=sheet+selected total allowance(.10–.30 mm). Foot tongue length=4.50–allowance. Trial-print clean parts to calibrate running fits/first-layer expansion for your printer/material. No universal clearance claim.
4. Before attaching feet, pass the shoe's axle and10×6 mm neck up through the guide slot from BELOW. Shoe sole stays under guide. Place pencil arm over the round axle ABOVE the guide; pencil end points out past the front edge. Axial gaps .20 mm above and below the guide and below keeper. Slot is10+stroke+allowance by6+allowance; bore6+allowance mm. Do not force or glue moving surfaces.
5. Slip keeper over axle and glue to axle ONLY (.14 mm proposed diametric bond gap). Maintain .20 mm gap over arm with a temporary paper shim; remove shim after cure. Keeper is NOT self-retaining without adhesive. Glue four foot tongues into guide sockets with both broad feet flat on table. Cure square. Arm, shoe and guide must move freely before use. Carrier runners are not functional retainers.
6. Tape the guide to a firm surface with pencil paper in front. Hold a short pencil upright in its6.5–8.5 mm clearance bore; bore does not clamp the pencil. Keep arm pointing60–120° toward paper. Hand-drag the shoe along the straight slot while allowing arm to trail and pencil friction to steer. The pivot spacing34–46 mm is a constant tow length. Reverse/lift to reset. Preview sliders show bounded rigid positions, NOT an automatic tractrix/friction simulation. A true tractrix additionally assumes ideal no-side-slip at the pencil; hand results depend on friction, play and technique. No precision claim.
7. No springs, bearings, fasteners, magnets or hidden hardware. External pencil, paper, removable tape and plastic-compatible adhesive required. Keeper capture depends on adhesive cure. Keep glue away from arm. Inspect before use; this is a gentle tabletop craft, not a precision drafting tool.
8. Local PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text and six bundled icons produce a20×20 sampled height-field engraving in the FRONT guide patch at(0,-13) mm, safe radius6.5 mm, .4–.6 mm deep, leaving≥1 mm backing. Bright holes remain uncut in solid backing. Fit/crop/background/threshold/relief and physical transforms change exported solid. This is2D-to-relief, not 3D photo reconstruction or exact tracing. Raised marks intentionally unsupported to keep the tow area unobstructed.
9. ASCII/binary STL≤2 MB /1500 triangles /.2–250 mm per axis; one closed outward convex broad-flat-bottom solid supported. Concave, open, hollow, multi-body, self-intersecting, overhanging meshes rejected, never hulled/repaired silently. Confirm mm, choose up axis before import; proportional XY scale, independent depth, rotate and XY placement change actual exact-mesh Boolean engraving. Files stay local; portable designs contain bounded normalized assets.
