Peel & Spiral — five-part stationary winding-drum drawing kit.
1. Print at 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters. All parts bed-flat, no supports intended. Drum radius18–26 mm; flange overhang3–5; sheet1.6–2.4. Use clean-part trial prints to calibrate fit before a full card.
2. Snip ALL20 accessible thin runners (four per part), not the functional two4.5 mm tongues. File burrs and elephant foot. Export clean parts instead when uncertain. Cutter blades kept outside all part boundaries by design.
3. Socket dimensions4.50×(sheet+allowance); tongue(4.50-allowance)×sheet mm. Total allowance .10–.30, not per side; source measured4.50×1.700012 on1.6 sheet. Printer/material dependent, not universal. Dry-seat two tongues in plinth square for upright storage. Keep sockets UNGLUED so the spool can lift out for drawing. This is a loose support, not positive retention.
4. Place winding drum over rear-flange Ø6 axle, gap .10 mm to rear face. Glue drum to shaft/rear flange, keeping glue off circumference. Drum boreØ(6+allowance). Tie a compact knot in0.5–0.8 mm soft low-stretch cord. Place knot inØ2.8 pocket and lead cord through1.2 mm radial exit BEFORE bonding front flange over axle (Ø6.14 glue bore, .10 mm layer gap). All spool parts are stationary and glued, no hidden friction retention.
5. Flanges extend3–5 mm beyond the96-sided drum. Feed only one single-layer turn of cord in the channel; no crossings. Avoid peeling across the anchor slot. Start with a trailing tangent and hand-pull taut in the drum's vertical plane. Ideal centerline radius R has involute x=R(cosθ+θsinθ),y=R(sinθ-θcosθ); released length Rθ. This describes the cord endpoint, not a dynamically simulated pencil.
6. For tabletop paper tracing, remove spool from its upright plinth and lay the REAR flange flat on paper. Secure it with removable tape. The pencil wand holds a short pencil loosely in its6.5–8.5 mm bore, with a2.4 mm cord eye16 mm away. Hold pencil vertical and let wand follow the cord tangent. The pencil curve is OFFSET from the ideal cord-end involute by16 mm, depends on wand orientation/play and is not a precision construction. An optional direct cord tie around pencil removes most wand offset. Do not claim exact drawn involutes. Upright pose is storage/explanation; drawing pose is horizontal.
7. External0.5–0.8 mm soft cord (~400 mm), pencil, paper, removable tape and compatible adhesive required. No metal hardware. Do not tension hard, use as a load-bearing winch, or spin the glued spool. Fit, adhesion, cord tracking and physical printing/drawing remain UNTESTED.
8. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG/text/icons become a20×20 sampled triangulated height-field engraving in plinth patch(0,-12), radius6.5, depth.4–.6 mm, backing≥1 mm. Fit/crop, background, threshold/relief, rotation/scale/placement affect actual Boolean solid. Bright holes stay uncut. Not full3D reconstruction or exact contour tracing. Raised marks unsupported here to preserve flat thickness.
9. ASCII/binary STL≤2 MB/1500triangles/.2–250 mm each axis: one outward closed convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Open, nonmanifold, concave, multi-body, hollow, overhanging or self-intersecting sources rejected. Never silently hulled or repaired. Confirm mm; up-axis chosen before import; proportional XY footprint and independentZ depth used in exact-mesh Boolean engraving. Everything processes locally. Portable JSON holds bounded normalized assets.
10. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Original reconstruction of measured sheet/socket/carrier principles; drum, knot pocket, layered spool and involute interaction are NEW. Proposed .4–.6 mm runners replace measured .21–.22. Raw fan-art mesh is not redistributed. Computational mesh/assembly tests are not physical testing. STL contains no colors.
