Keel & Trace — three-part Prytz-inspired heel-following kit card.
1. Print at100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers,3 perimeters. All three parts and carrier bed-flat at Z0; no supports intended. Sheet1.6–2.4 mm. Use clean-part trials for printer/material calibration.
2. Snip ALL12 accessible runners (four per part). Snip at1.6 mm outside the part, then trim/file remaining nubs; do not cut the four functional tongues. Individual-part exports omit the carrier/runners.
3. Source-derived sockets4.50 × (sheet+allowance) mm; tongues(4.50-allowance) × sheet. Total allowance .10–.30 mm, not per side. Source measured4.50×1.700012 on1.6 sheet. Dry-fit; file elephant foot or increase allowance, never force. These are loose adhesive seats, not snap locks.
4. Keep the signature beam horizontal. Insert directional heel UP into the two sockets at the opposite end from the round pencil bore until its shoulders touch the beam underside. The long .8 mm paper edge runs parallel to the pencil-to-heel axis. Lower the pierced grip into the other two sockets from ABOVE; its shoulders rest on beam top. Bond all four fixed seats and cure. No press-fit retention claim.
5. Hold a pencil vertically through the6.5–9 mm bore, adjust its exposed tip until both pencil and heel touch paper. The bore is a loose guide, not a pencil clamp. Rest fingers on grip; do not tilt or force the heel. External pencil, paper, compatible adhesive and optionally a ruler are required. No bearings, magnets or fasteners.
6. Trace a small gentle closed contour with the pencil, starting with heel clear of the boundary. The18 mm elongated heel is intended to prefer sliding along the beam and resist lateral skid. Record its initial/final location with a second pencil. Vary span70–100 mm and compare drift. A smooth plastic heel may skid on some papers; surface friction, force and accuracy must be experimentally calibrated. Do not use sharp metal blades.
7. This is inspired by a Prytz/hatchet planimeter, not a precision instrument. For suitably small contours and a prescribed tracing protocol, ideal nonholonomic theory relates area to span×lateral drift approximately; the app does NOT compute area, simulate friction or assert accuracy. Reversing trace direction or heel placement changes error. Assembled/exploded views explain the actual geometry, not measured tracking.
8. Images/text/icons/simple SVG become a20×20 sampled triangulated height-field engraving in protected beam patch(0,-8), radius6.5 mm. Threshold or grayscale, crop/fit, background, scale, rotation and placement alter real Boolean geometry. Depth .4–.6 leaves≥1 mm continuous backing. Bright holes remain uncut; not exact contour tracing or full3D reconstruction from one image. Raised marks unsupported to preserve bed-flat sheet.
9. ASCII/binary STL≤2 MB/1500triangles/.2–250 mm per axis: one outward closed convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Open, nonmanifold, concave, multi-body, hollow, overhanging or self-intersecting inputs rejected, never hulled or silently repaired. Confirm mm (STL has no units); up-axis before import. Proportional XY scale and independentZ depth fit the exact source into a Boolean engraving, away from sockets. Local browser processing only. Bounded normalized assets travel in JSON; personal files are never uploaded or committed.
10. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Measured sheet/socket/carrier principles reconstructed originally; tracer beam, opposite-direction feet, elongated bevel heel and pencil guide are NEW. Proposed .4–.6 mm runners replace source .21–.22. No raw third-party mesh is redistributed. Computational checks are not physical printing, friction, wear or tracing tests. STL stores no colours.
