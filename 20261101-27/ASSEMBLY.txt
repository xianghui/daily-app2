Grid & Gaze — three-part perspective-drawing window kit card.
1. Print at 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters. Window, two feet and carrier are bed-flat at Z0; no supports intended. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm; grid ribs 1.2–2 mm. Print clean-part samples first to calibrate material and fit.
2. Snip ALL 12 accessible runners (four per part). Snip 1.6 mm outside each part and trim/file remaining nubs. Avoid the two bottom tongues and the thin grid ribs. Clean-part STLs omit tabs and frame.
3. Measured reference: 1.60 mm sheet and 4.50 × 1.700012 mm socket. Reconstructed foot sockets are 4.50 × (sheet+allowance) mm; window tongues are (4.50-allowance) × sheet. Total allowance .10–.30 mm is not per side. File elephant foot or adjust allowance. These are adhesive seats, not snap locks.
4. Stand both feet flat with their sockets aligned. Hold the window upright with its wide signature rail at the bottom. Lower both tongues together into the sockets until the bottom rail shoulders touch foot tops. Check square, dry-fit and bond both seats; allow compatible adhesive to cure. No hardware, bearings or magnets. Adhesive, paper, pencil and a stable viewing setup are external.
5. Set the window on a level table between one eye and a small object. Keep your head fixed; close the other eye. Match the chosen columns/rows and aperture aspect ratio on paper, then transfer visible edges cell by cell. Printed ribs obscure narrow strips of the view; this is an observation aid, not a transparent image plane or lens. Do not look at the sun or bright lamps.
6. Width 70–96 mm; clear height 36–52 mm; 3–6 rows/columns. Actual aperture width is outer width minus 12 mm. Grid changes affect real closed solid geometry. Feet 24 ×32 mm; assembled height = clear height+24+sheet. Center of mass remains within the rectangular foot support envelope in the nominal upright pose; this is not a tipping/load test.
7. Corner silhouettes: square, 5 mm rounded, or clipped. These change the outer rail/feet, not the interior grid's optical spacing. All exact assembly views use the same three solids as the exports; exploded view lifts only the window. No motion is simulated.
8. PNG/JPEG/text/icons/simple SVG become a 20×20 sampled triangulated height-field engraving in a protected radius6.5 mm patch at (0,9) on the lower rail, clear of grid and tongues. Threshold/grayscale, fit/crop, background, scale, rotation and placement change actual Boolean cuts. Depth .4–.6 leaves at least1 mm backing. White holes remain uncut, with sampled/sloping boundary interpolation. This is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Raised marks unsupported to retain bed-flat sheet envelope.
9. ASCII/binary STL ≤2 MB/1500 triangles/.2–250 mm per axis: one outward closed convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Invalid/open/nonmanifold/concave/hollow/multibody/overhanging meshes are rejected, never hulled or repaired. Confirm mm because STL has no units. Select up-axis before attaching; proportional XY scale and independent Z depth fit the original exact mesh into a Boolean engraving. All personal files stay local. JSON/named designs carry bounded normalized payloads; missing files produce a reattachment error. STL stores no colours.
10. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Actual STL parsed and sectioned; source sheet, socket and breakaway carrier principles informed this original reconstruction. Proposed runners .4–.6 mm replace .21–.22 mm source webs. Source spacecraft/fan-art outlines are not reproduced and raw third-party mesh is not bundled. Geometry and assembly checks are computational; no physical print, adhesive, tipping or drawing test has been performed.
