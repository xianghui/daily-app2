Suspend & Centre — four-part centre-of-mass suspension kit card.
1. Print at 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters. Use 100% infill for the lamina and bob to approximate the uniform-material centroid. All four parts and carrier start at Z0; no supports intended. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm. Keep parts away from children: small parts and cords.
2. Snip all 16 accessible runners, four per part. Cut 1.6 mm outside each contour and file nubs. Never cut the two portal tongues, three lamina eyes or cord eye. De-tabbed STLs omit the carrier and runners.
3. Measured reference sheet is 1.60 mm with 4.50 ×1.700012 mm sockets. New base slots are 4.50 ×(sheet+allowance); portal tongues are (4.50-allowance) ×sheet. Total allowance .10–.30 mm, not per side. These are loose adhesive seats, not snap locks. Print the base and portal samples first; adjust fit or file elephant foot.
4. Lower the portal straight down into both rear base sockets until shoulders touch the base. Square, dry-fit then bond both seats with compatible adhesive and let cure. There is no claimed friction retention. No bearings, magnets or screws.
5. External supplies: one straight Ø2 ×30 mm bamboo rod/skewer, two thin soft threads, paper tape, fine pencil and adhesive. Glue the rod horizontally through the Ø2.4 mm portal eye, perpendicular to the sheet. Leave20 mm projecting toward the base front and10 mm behind the portal plane. Let glue cure. Tie the short lamina thread10 mm forward of the portal; tie the bob thread15 mm forward. Both threads now hang vertically in separate parallel planes, with the bob in front. Glue the knots to the rod to prevent sliding. Tie lamina eye A, then adjust the bob thread to hang a few mm above the base. Exact knot lengths are maker-adjusted; ghost rod and cord lengths are illustrative and not exported.
6. Put removable paper tape on the plate. Let it settle and mark the plumb line from the selected eye. Rehang from eye B, then C; repeat. The gravity lines should approach a common centre. The centre shown is a signed-tetrahedron centroid of the real exported lamina mesh, including its off-centre opening and three eyes, under a homogeneous-solid assumption. Slicer density, cord forces and pen/tape mass alter the result. It is not a calibrated weighing instrument.
7. Change lamina width34–44 mm, off-centre opening radius4–7 mm and portal height72–92 mm. These alter real geometry; the hole shifts the calculated centroid. Rounded, square-edge and clipped silhouettes alter actual base/plate contours. Selected-eye preview shows ideal gravity alignment, not dynamic motion or tested equilibrium.
8. PNG/JPEG/text/icons/simple SVG become a 20×20 sampled triangulated height-field engraving in a protected radius6.5 mm patch at (0,9) on the base, away from sockets and suspended parts. Threshold/grayscale, fit/crop, background, scale, rotation and placement affect Boolean cuts. Depth .4–.6 mm leaves at least1 mm backing. White/transparent holes remain uncut with sampled/sloping interpolation. This is 2D-to-relief, not single-photo full3D reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Raised marks unsupported to preserve a constant sheet envelope.
9. ASCII/binary STL ≤2 MB/1500 triangles/.2–250 mm per axis: one outward closed convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Invalid/open/nonmanifold/concave/hollow/multibody/overhanging meshes are rejected, never hulled or repaired. Confirm mm because STL has no units. Select up-axis before attaching. XY proportionally scales to4–7 mm footprint; Z independently scales to.4–.6 mm engraving depth. The exact source mesh is Boolean-subtracted into the base. All personal inputs stay local. JSON/named designs carry bounded normalized payloads; missing files request reattachment. STL stores no colour.
10. Reference: White Cat Kit Card, Mike Kasberg, Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Actual mesh parsed and sectioned. Measured sheet/socket and removable carrier structure inform this original reconstruction; no fan-art shape or raw third-party mesh is redistributed. Proposed bed-level runners.4–.6 mm replace source low webs.21–.22 mm. Computational mesh/clearance/assembly checks are not physical printing, adhesive, cord-friction, fatigue, tipping or measurement tests; none of those physical tests was performed.
