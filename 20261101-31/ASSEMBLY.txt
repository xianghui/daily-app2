Sway & Share — four-part coupled-pendulum kit card.
1. Print at 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters. Use matching solid infill for both bobs. All parts/carrier start at Z0. No supports intended. Small parts/cords: adult craft model, not a child toy or load-bearing device.
2. Snip all16 accessible runners, four per part,1.6 mm outside each contour; file nubs. Keep two portal tongues, two top eyes and both bob eyes intact. Clean-part STLs omit tabs.
3. Source: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card, Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Parsed source sheet1.60 mm and sockets4.50×1.700012 mm. New portal tongues(4.50-fit)×sheet, base slots4.50×(sheet+fit); fit.10–.30 mm TOTAL, not per side. Original socket/carrier structure informed a wholly different coupled-pendulum assembly. No raw source mesh is bundled. Source low webs.21–.22 mm replaced by proposed bed-level.4–.6 mm runners.
4. Dry-fit the two portal tongues vertically into the rear base sockets, shoulder-to-top. Print these two parts as a calibration pair first; adjust allowance/file elephant foot. Bond both loose seats with compatible adhesive, square the portal and let cure. Not snap locks or guaranteed friction fits.
5. External supplies: two Ø2×20 mm bamboo dowels, one flexible coupling cord, two matching bob cords, adhesive and removable ruler tape. Glue dowels through the Ø2.4 mm top eyes. Portal front plane is Y30; front tips are Y18,12 mm ahead. Keep support cord just in front of the dowel tips, secured against slipping, at Z(sheet+portal height-4). Flexible middle string sags roughly4 mm; exact sag and preview cord paths are illustrative and maker-adjusted, not exported geometry.
6. Tie bob cords to the shared string at X=±(spacing/2). Equal effective drop30–42 mm is measured from support knot to bob eye centre, including knot effects. Do not tie individual bob cords directly to the rigid beam; that removes the intended flexible coupling. Tie the bobs through their Ø2.2 mm eyes and secure knots. With the support sag near4 mm the bobs clear the base. Adjust both lengths equally until they swing at similar rates.
7. Move one bob gently forward/back,≤5°, release without pushing the other. Watch for gradual energy exchange through the yielding support string, not collisions. Keep the base taped down; damping, support tension, filament mass and unequal knots can prevent a visible exchange. This is not a clock, precision resonance instrument, tested fidget or simulation. No performance/transfer-time claim. Static preview positions and cords are an assembly explanation only. Check clearance by hand before use; reduce amplitude if cords slip or bobs approach the portal/base.
8. Actual geometry controls: spacing28–40 mm changes bob plan positions; portal height72–92 mm, bob radius5–9 mm and silhouettes change actual solids. Cord drop30–42 mm changes the assembly cord-length plan only (external cords, not an STL property). Card160–210×170–220 mm, sheet1.6–2.4 mm, part gap3–5 mm; tab width.8–1.4 and height.4–.6 mm.
