Cross & Sight — three-part crossed-slit viewing kit card.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale, opaque PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers and 3 perimeters. All parts and runners start at Z0; no supports intended. Small adult craft model, not an eye-protection device.
2. Snip twelve runners, four per part, 1.6 mm outside each contour; file nubs. Preserve all four tongues and both apertures. Clean-part STLs omit tabs.
3. Source: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card, Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0. Parsed sheet 1.60 mm; socket loops 4.50×1.700012 mm. Reconstructed slots 4.50×(sheet+allowance), tongues (4.50-allowance)×sheet. Allowance .10–.30 mm TOTAL, not per side. Original carrier/socket structure informs a different crossed-slit assembly. No raw mesh bundled. Source low webs .21–.22 mm replaced with proposed .4–.6 mm bed-aligned runners.
4. Dry-fit vertical gate into the FRONT pair of sockets at Y20, horizontal gate into REAR pair at Y20+separation. Lower both until shoulders meet the base top. Print base and one gate as a calibration pair first; adjust allowance or file elephant foot. Bond all four loose seats square with compatible adhesive. Cure before use. These are not snap locks or guaranteed friction fits.
5. Set on a desk. Look from behind the horizontal slit along the base through both apertures at a nearby high-contrast scene. Move your head to explore independent horizontal/vertical sight constraints and baseline-dependent parallax. This open viewer does not form an image on a screen. Optional black tape can reduce stray light; keep both apertures unobstructed.
6. Computational validation checks each closed outward-wound solid, single connected carrier, runner removal, card bounds, bed support, aperture probes, gate insertion paths and assembled clearances. It does NOT establish optical quality, print strength or physical fit. No physical print or optical performance test has been performed. Never point at the sun, laser or intense light. No lenses, switches, bearings or magnets required; adhesive is required.
7. Imports are local 2D-to-engraved relief or exact convex-STL Boolean cuts in the protected base patch, not reconstruction from a photo. 20×20 sampled height grid; holes remain as uncut backing. Source/final previews and all exports share current valid geometry. STL has no units or preview colors. ZIP includes card and clean parts plus these instructions.
