Ames & Turn — six-part rotating trapezoid window kit card.
1. Print all pieces bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, .4 mm nozzle and 3 perimeters. No supports intended. The default carrier is 240×260 mm; use individual STLs for smaller beds. Do not scale globally: change parameters to preserve fit.
2. Clip 24 accessible runners with flush cutters, four per piece. Cut 1.6 mm outside each contour and file nubs. Preserve axles, tongues, panes and keeper. Small adult craft model; not a toy for young children.
3. Attribution: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0. Parsed sheet planes0–1.60 mm and 4.50×1.700012 mm sockets inform this original sheet/socket/carrier reconstruction. Slots are4.50×(sheet+allowance); tongues(4.50-allowance)×sheet. Allowance .10–.30 mm TOTAL. Measured source .21–.22mm low webs are redesigned as .4–.6mm bed-aligned runners. Raw source is not redistributed.
4. Calibrate with base, one pillar and keeper first. Dry-fit four pillar seats and the keeper on an axle. File elephant foot or adjust fit; never force. Bond the two pillars into the base. Keep the center blind bearing clean and unglued.
5. Lower the window bottom axle into the base blind bearing, whose floor is0.8mm. The printed 4.50×sheet rectangular axle rotates in a 64-sided circular bore sized to its circumradius plus the chosen radial clearance. This is a low-speed hand-driven journal, not a precision bearing.
6. Slide lintel DOWN over the window's top axle and both pillar tongues together. Bond only the two pillar seats. After curing, slide the separate keeper onto the exposed top axle. Set its underside0.30mm ABOVE the lintel using a temporary paper/shim gauge. Apply a tiny amount of compatible adhesive ONLY inside its rectangular axle seat, cure and remove the shim. Keeper must remain bonded: friction alone is NOT positive retention. Do not wick glue into the rotating bore. No screws, magnets, purchased bearings or switches are needed; compatible adhesive is required.
7. Hold the base level. Gently hand-turn the frame at the mullion. Look with one eye from roughly the front at eye level, using a plain backdrop. The asymmetric trapezoid can appear to rock instead of rotate: perception varies, and this is not guaranteed for every observer. The preview pose is a rigid exact geometric rotation, not an optical rendering or physical dynamics simulation.
8. Collision checks sample the full360° in5° steps plus insertion/lift paths; analytical circumradius bounds cover intervening angles. Lower axle stays engaged through the0.30mm keeper travel. Keeper overlap prevents withdrawal only while its adhesive bond remains sound. Base tipping, journal friction, adhesive strength, visual illusion, physical print and fatigue are NOT tested. Stop if binding; recalibrate rather than force.
9. Imports engrave only the protected front base patch, away from journals, slots and moving window. PNG/JPEG/SVG are sampled20×20 2D height reliefs, not full3D reconstruction.
