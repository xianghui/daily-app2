Notch & Code — seven-part three-slider interlock teaching kit.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, .4 mm nozzle, three perimeters. All pieces print without intended supports. Frame is sheet+0.25mm thick, giving 0.25mm TOTAL vertical room above sliders. Default connected carrier240×280mm; use clean-part STL plate on smaller beds.
2. Clip28 accessible runners with flush cutters, four per piece; cut1.6mm outside contours and file nubs. Do not cut inside functional notches. Adult craft model, small parts; not a security lock or child restraint.
3. Attribution: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card (CC BY4.0). Parsed sheet0–1.60mm, carrier cross-sections and .21–.22mm low webs informed this original reconstruction. Redesign uses .4–.6mm bed-aligned tabs. Measured4.50×1.700012mm sockets are documented but not falsely reused as sliding clearances; the4.50mm cover finger apertures retain that nominal span only. Raw STL not redistributed.
4. Print floor, frame and gate-one first as a calibration set. Tune per-side XY running clearance .20–.40mm, clean elephant foot and dry-run. Vertical gap is a proposed .25mm TOTAL; if your printer closes it, sand mating faces or use slicer horizontal/elephant-foot compensation. Fits depend on printer/material; no universal fit claim.
5. Bond guide-frame to floor aligned on the48mm-wide upper rectangle, leaving the20mm signature apron exposed. Let cure. Place three gates in their top/middle/bottom pockets, ridges upward and notches facing right. Gate-one is the lower-Y station in the preview (bottom on a top-down print); gate-two center; gate-three upper. Their notch offsets encode0/1/2 as -3/0/+3mm. The solution shifts each gate by the negative of its notch offset.
6. Place comb bolt in its right-hand guide, ridges upward and teeth facing left. With gates aligned, comb travels4mm left; otherwise at least one finger meets a gate. Return bolt fully right before changing gates. Teeth bottom out at a0.5mm clearance from notch backs. Never force blocked parts.
7. Lay window-cover down from above. Bond ONLY to fixed frame margins, keeping glue away from all four moving pieces and guide pockets. Temporary clips/tape help hold alignment while curing. Three4.50mm-wide windows expose gate ridges. Center right window exposes the bolt. Cover overlap and cavity ends retain parts only after the adhesive cures; no snap-fit/friction retention claim.
8. This is a visible-code mechanical AND-gate demonstration, not a secure combination lock. No magnets, bearings, screws or switches; compatible adhesive required at floor/frame/cover. Notch codes, station pitch, tooth width and running gap alter the real exported geometry. Assembled/exploded/engagement preview is rigid placement, not force or friction simulation.
9. Imports engrave only the exposed floor apron, outside guides and strokes. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG become sampled20×20 thresholded/grayscale2D relief cutters, not automatic3D reconstruction. Bright holes leave full-height backing; engraving .4–.6mm leaves at least1mm. Verified convex flat-base ASCII/binary STL is exact Boolean-subtracted with explicit mm confirmation and transforms. Unsupported concave/open/self-intersecting meshes are rejected, never hull-replaced. Local processing only. ZIP contains connected card, seven clean parts and these instructions.

Physical printing, friction, glue strength, wear and fatigue are untested. Stop on binding and calibrate rather than force. STL contains no colours or unit metadata.
