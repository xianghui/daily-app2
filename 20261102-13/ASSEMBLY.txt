Duplex & Decode — three-part visual-cryptography kit card.
1. Print at 100% mm, opaque dark PLA/PETG, .16–.20mm layers, .4mm nozzle, three perimeters. All parts start on Z=0; ledge supports every raised cradle wall. Default card300×150mm needs a wide bed; clean parts are much smaller. No intended supports, metal hardware or glue.
2. Clip12 runners, four per part, at marked1.6mm outboard snip positions. File the remaining nubs to the silhouette. Do not nick small apertures or the cradle pocket. Adult desk/craft kit with small cutoffs, not a toy for young children.
3. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Parsed1.60mm sheet, subdivided carrier and.21–.22mm low webs informed original reconstruction. New bed-aligned.4–.6mm runners are thicker for FDM handling. Source4.50×1.700012mm sockets are documented, not repurposed as optical fits. Raw STL not redistributed.
4. Calibrate by printing the cradle and share-a first. Remove elephant foot. Per-side pocket gap.15–.35mm depends on printer/material; default.20mm is a starting point, not universal. The masks must lower without force and sit level on the ledge. Apertures are1.20–1.70mm and webs.80mm; use dry opaque filament and inspect every hole.
5. Put cradle aperture over a diffuse bright background (daylit paper or a frosted lamp, never the sun/laser). Drop share-a from above onto the ledge at Z=sheet. Its small orientation eye is at the upper-left corner. Place share-b directly atop it with the same eye at upper-left; its underside is at Z=2×sheet. Both are intentionally removable, not positively retained. No snap-fit or friction-retention claim.
6. Each cell has four possible square sub-apertures. Share-a opens exactly two chosen by the seed. Share-b opens the same pair for a light cell, or its complement for a dark cell. In the stacked vertical projection a light cell has two clear subpixels and a dark cell has none. Neither share alone reveals the symbol in its per-cell open area. This is deterministic teaching geometry, NOT secure cryptography or a verified random one-time pad.
7. Separate the masks and compare their equal-density patterns. Stack and align: a heart/ring/bolt appears dark against a half-open background in the ideal vertical ray model. Lift the top share at the finger notch, misregister it in free space to compare, then reseat. The preview slider lifts the top share vertically; it does not assert that the mask slides through the rim. View near perpendicular; thick perforations vignette oblique light, and FDM defects can reduce contrast. No optical test or printed prototype has been performed.
8. Aperture grid, cell count, seed, sheet thickness, pocket gap and tray silhouettes alter exported solids. Assembled/exploded/isolated/connected-card/de-tabbed views use the same parts. The checkerboarded cutout is local2D heightfield conversion, not automatic reconstruction from a photo.
9. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG are sampled to20×20 threshold or grayscale heightfield cutters on the front cradle apron, outside pocket and light window. Bright holes keep full backing; .4–.6mm engraving leaves≥1mm. Verified convex flat-bottom ASCII/binary STL is exact Boolean-subtracted with explicit mm confirmation, proportional XY footprint, independent Z depth, rotation and placement. Unsupported concave/open/hollow/overhang meshes are rejected, not hull-replaced or claimed repaired. Inputs remain local; bounded assets travel inside design JSON.
10. ZIP contains connected card, three clean part STLs and this guide with current fits. STL contains no colour or units. Computational checks cover manifold edges/winding, dimensions, snip release, assembly insertion, pocket gaps, no overlaps, support footprints and ideal projected Boolean decoding for every cell. Physical printing, friction, toughness, optical contrast and wear remain untested.
