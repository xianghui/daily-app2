Pento & Pack — thirteen-part pentomino kit card.
1. Print at 100% mm in PLA/PETG, .16–.20mm layers, .4mm nozzle, three perimeters. All parts start on Z=0. The continuous tray floor supports its raised rim. Default card300×160mm requires a wide bed; alternatively export the individual clean parts or de-tabbed plate and arrange in your slicer. No supports, hardware, glue or switches intended.
2. Clip52 runners, four per component, at the1.6mm-outboard snip positions. File all remaining nubs carefully to the original silhouette. Do not gouge tile edges or the tray pocket. This desk/craft kit has small pieces and sharp cutoffs; adult supervision for cutting, not for young children.
3. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Measured1.60mm sheets, subdivided carrier and.21–.22mm webs informed this original reconstruction. New bed-aligned.4–.6mm runners are deliberately thicker. Measured4.50×1.700012mm slots are documented but not incorrectly imposed on a loose tile puzzle. Raw reference STL not bundled.
4. Print the tray and tile-P first as a fit trial. Remove elephant foot. Tile-to-tile gap.20–.40mm and outer tile-to-wall gap of the same value depend on printer/material. Default.30mm is only a starting point. Five-cell tile outlines are inset by half the chosen gap; this leaves equal inter-tile separation in a nominal full-grid packing. Each tile is at least5.6mm across its narrowest neck. There are no press fits or claimed universal tolerances.
5. Release all thirteen parts. Place tray floor-down. Identify twelve free pentominoes F,I,L,P,N,T,U,V,W,X,Y,Z from their silhouettes in the assembly map. Each consists of five edge-connected square lattice cells; rotations and face flips are permitted. This is a physical packing set generator, not a browser puzzle game.
6. Lower tiles one by one vertically into the open tray, following the optional solved assembly map. All twelve fit in the chosen6×10,5×12 or4×15 rectangle. There is no locking assembly order or undercut. The front rim notch gives access to lift tiles. They are loose, not retained for carrying upside down. The UI lift is a rigid preview-only displacement, not a physical motion mechanism or a new export orientation.
7. Try reassembling the set without the solved map, or compare the three different rectangles by generating matching trays. Cell pitch, tray aspect, sheet thickness, silhouette, clearance and carrier dimensions alter actual exported solids. Different tray presets may rotate or mirror a tile's print orientation; the underlying free pentomino shapes remain equivalent. Shape colours help distinguish pieces but STL does not store colour.
8. Text, bundled icons, PNG/JPEG/simple SVG art engrave only the protected front apron, away from the packing surface. Image conversion is a20×20 threshold/grayscale sampled heightfield, not full3D reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Bright holes preserve solid backing; .4–.6mm engraving leaves≥1mm. Use crop/fit, threshold, scale, rotation and position controls; checkerboard shows the actual cutout.
9. Verified closed convex flat-bottom ASCII/binary STL is used as an exact Boolean engraving cutter after explicit millimeter confirmation. XY footprint scales proportionally; Z is independently fitted to.4–.6mm depth. Unsupported concave/open/hollow/overhang input is rejected, never hull-replaced, repaired without evidence or merely displayed. Inputs stay in this browser; bounded portable payloads restore through JSON and named designs.
10. ZIP contains the connected card, tray, twelve clean tile STLs and these instructions with current fits. Computational verification covers every solid's manifold edges, winding, positive volume, float32 exports, exact60-cell cover, pairwise assembled collisions, vertical insertion, floor support, card bounds, accessible runner cuts and fourteen components after snipping. Physical printing, friction, fit and durability have NOT been tested.
