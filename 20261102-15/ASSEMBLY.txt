Log & Slide — two-part logarithmic slide-rule kit card.
1. Print at 100% mm in PLA/PETG, .16–.20mm layers, .4mm nozzle and three perimeters. All parts bed-flat at Z=0. No supports, hardware or adhesive intended. Print body + slide first as a fit trial; preview shows no captive retention.
2. Clip EIGHT accessible low runners at the outboard snip positions (1.6mm away from parts). File remaining nubs flush. Avoid engraving and channel walls. Cutting tools and small cutoffs require supervision; not a young-child toy.
3. Drop the clean slide vertically into the open-ended channel. The chosen .20–.40mm gap is PER SIDE, printer/material dependent; remove elephant foot. Floor supports the slide. It may be lifted out or slid off an end; keep the kit flat. No snap fit, press fit or retained transport is claimed.
4. The lower fixed D scale and upper moving C scale use x = L log10(v), from 1 to the doubled terminal decade index. Numerals 1–9 and finer ticks are actual .4mm-deep engravings, not preview artwork. Fill recessed marks manually if contrast is needed; STL contains no colour. Minimum tick width .45mm; labels are seven-segment numerals.
5. Multiply 2 × 3: set C's left index (1) over D's 2; beneath C's 3, read D's 6. More generally log(a)+log(b)=log(a*b). If the result runs past the end, use the other decade index or adjust the decimal mentally. It is a qualitative educational rule, not a certified measuring tool. Divide by reversing this alignment. Preview shift 0–60% of scale is display-only, not a new export orientation.
6. The body apron accepts text/icons, PNG/JPEG/simple SVG as a locally sampled 20×20 threshold/grayscale engraving, not automatic full3D reconstruction. Holes retain backing. ASCII/binary STL is an exact Boolean engraving cutter after explicit mm confirmation: one verified outward closed convex flat-bottom solid only. Unsupported concave/open/hollow/overhang meshes are rejected, never replaced with hulls. Portable JSON and named saves carry bounded assets.
7. Reference: Mike Kasberg's White Cat Kit Card, Printables865769, github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Measured 1.60mm sheets, segmented carrier rails and .21–.22mm webs inform the original carrier reconstruction. Our .4–.6mm bed-aligned runners are deliberately thicker; measured source 4.5×1.7mm slots do NOT apply to this loose slider.
8. ZIP includes connected-card, signature-body, logarithmic-slide and these instructions with current dimensions. Computational checks are separate from physical tests: no physical print, friction, breakaway force, accuracy, legibility or durability test has been performed.
