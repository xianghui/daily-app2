Pascal & Align — four-part projective geometry kit card.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20mm layers, .4mm nozzle, three perimeters. No supports intended. First print one foot and the board as a fit trial; sockets use total (not per-side) clearance .10–.35mm, depending on printer and material.
2. Clip SIXTEEN accessible bed-aligned runners, 1.6mm outboard from clean part boundaries. File all nubs flush. Keep cutters away from the two lower board tongues, slots and engravings. Small cutoffs require supervision; not a child toy.
3. Stand both feet floor-down with slots aligned side-by-side. Lower the board's two 4.50mm-wide tongues vertically into the slots until its bottom shoulders rest on the foot tops. Tongue engagement equals sheet thickness. Center the board thickness over the feet. Fit is intentionally loose; BOND BOTH SOCKETS with a small amount of compatible adhesive and let it cure. No snap-lock or friction-only retention is promised. Avoid glue on the engraved face.
4. Six shallow wells lie on a circle; tally marks identify points 1–6 in crossing-hexagon order. Trace sides 1–2 and 4–5: their intersection is the first well near the centre. Similarly pair sides 2–3 / 5–6, and 3–4 / 6–1. These three intersection centres lie on one line by Pascal's theorem. The dashed line is calculated geometry, not a texture. Apply contrasting paint to recessed marks if desired; STL does not store colours.
5. Lift the separate sighting rule and hold its 1.20mm through-slot over the three intersection wells on the engraved face. Align all centres through the slot to check their straight line. The rule remains hand-held and removable, NOT a captive moving component. It is shown beside the stand in assembled view; exploded offsets are illustrative, not a simulation. Point asymmetry changes the actual six points, six chords, intersection wells and Pascal line together. It is an educational construction, not a certified measuring tool.
6. Text, six bundled icons and local PNG/JPEG/simple SVG produce a 20×20 sampled threshold/grayscale engraving in the upper right protected board apron; not 3D reconstruction from a photograph. Bright holes retain solid backing. Only engraved style .4–.6mm is supported, preserving ≥1mm backing. Exact ASCII/binary STL subtraction requires explicit mm confirmation: one closed, outward-wound, convex, broad-flat-bottom solid, ≤1,500 triangles. Unsupported concave/open/hollow/overhang meshes are rejected, never replaced with a hull. Transforms affect the exported solid.
7. Structural reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables865769, github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Measured sheet 1.60mm, sockets 4.50×1.70mm and carrier web planes .21–.22mm. Original reconstruction uses 4.50mm tongues, adjustable sheet+clearance slots, segmented carrier and stronger .4–.6mm bed-aligned runners. No original artwork or source mesh is bundled.
8. ZIP contains connected-card, signature-body, left-foot, right-foot, sighting-rule STL plus these instructions and current fits. All coordinates are mm. Mesh/assembly computations do not prove a physical print: printing, fit, stability, breakaway force, legibility and hand-held use have not been physically tested.
