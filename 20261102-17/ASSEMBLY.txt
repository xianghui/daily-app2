Siphon & Crest — four-part flat-pack tube-routing kit.
1. Print all parts bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale: PLA/PETG, .4mm nozzle, .16–.20mm layers, three walls. No supports intended. Calibrate one tongue/socket first; clearance .10–.35mm TOTAL is printer-dependent. Sheet 1.6–2.4mm. Gauge holes are tube OD, OD+.2, OD+.4mm (one/two/three tally marks). Choose a slip fit, never squeeze a hose bore closed.
2. Clip SIXTEEN bed-aligned runners 1.6mm outboard of clean boundaries. File nubs flush. Keep snips away from tongues and eyes. Small cutoffs and tools need supervision.
3. Put base flat, engraving toward you. Lower the wide curved saddle's two tongues into the paired left sockets; lower the narrower outlet mast into the right socket. Shoulders seat on the base top. Use compatible adhesive in all THREE joints and let cure. The gauge stays loose. No friction-only retention is promised.
4. EXTERNAL supplies: flexible clear tubing 4–5mm outside diameter, about 0.6m long; four soft thread ties; two open water vessels, tray, absorbent cloth and compatible adhesive. Verify actual tube minimum bend radius with its maker. This saddle offers centerline radius at least four times OD, a starting assumption, NOT a universal kink guarantee. No printed part stores water or seals a tube.
5. Wrap the hose over the semicircular upper edge, centered on the sheet thickness; loosely tie it through the three 2.6mm eyes. Tube follows the OUTSIDE curve; leave the bore round and uncrushed. Route the downstream leg through the outlet mast's open U notch and secure loosely through its eye. Keep tube ends external and visibly open. The preview does not simulate a tube, water or gravity.
6. Work only with room-temperature water over a tray. Raise the supply vessel on a stable support so its surface is BELOW the hose crest but ABOVE the outlet tip; keep intake submerged. Fill the tube completely with water by immersion, cover both ends with fingers, place the intake in the supply and outlet over a lower receiving vessel, then uncover. Never mouth-prime, use hot liquids or ingest fluid. Air leaks/bubbles, kinks, exposed intake or an outlet not below the supply surface stop flow. Siphon needs hydrostatic head and an unbroken water column, not capillary pumping. Tube centreline crest and outlet saddle heights are displayed; external vessel levels and tip height are user-set, not mechanically guaranteed. No measured flow rate is claimed.
7. Personal art is an actual .4–.6mm engraving in the front base apron, away from sockets. PNG/JPEG/SVG use a 20×20 sampled threshold/grayscale height grid; bright holes keep backing. Not full 3D reconstruction. ASCII/binary STL is an exact Boolean engraving, with explicit mm confirmation. Only one verified closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom solid, up to 1500 triangles, is supported; no convex-hull substitution or automatic repair. All files stay local.
8. Structural reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables 865769, github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Measured 1.60mm sheet, 4.50×1.70mm sockets, .21–.22mm carrier webs. This original reconstruction retains 4.50mm tongues and adjusts socket width/thickness with calibration clearance; stronger .4–.6mm bed-aligned runners. No raw source mesh/artwork is bundled.
9. ZIP: connected-card, signature-body, crest-saddle, outlet-mast, tube-gauge STLs and these instructions/current dimensions. All prints are bed-flat; assembled and exploded transforms do not change export orientation. STL has no color. No physical printing, tube fit, kink resistance, adhesive strength, snipping force, stability or water-flow experiment has been performed.
