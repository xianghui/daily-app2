Cavalieri & Slice — seven-part section-comparison kit card.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. All clean parts have one sheet thickness; no supports intended. Start with one spine and one shelf as a fit trial. Clearance .10–.35 mm TOTAL is printer/material dependent, not universal.
2. Clip TWENTY bed-aligned runners at least 1.5 mm outboard of clean boundaries. Four on base, four on each spine, two at each shelf stem end. File nubs flush without thinning tongues. Wear eye protection and supervise snips/small parts.
3. With the base flat, lower the left and right spine tongues into the two rear base sockets. Shoulders rest on the base top, tongues end at bed. Bond BOTH sockets with compatible adhesive and let cure. Width 4.50 mm; receiving slots (4.50+gap) by (sheet+gap).
4. From the front, slide the upper small disc's rear stem backwards into the upper LEFT spine slot; slide the lower larger disc into its lower slot. Keep both plates level and bond tongue/socket seams. The slots are sheet+gap tall; they are adhesive seats, not self-retaining snaps. Support shelves while curing. The model is a lightweight display, never load-bearing.
5. Fit the upper large-hole annulus in the upper RIGHT slot, then the lower small-hole annulus in the lower RIGHT slot. Bond these two joints and cure. Six glued joints total. Shelf stems are printed integrally, and sockets engage a sheet thickness. No hardware, moving joint or untested friction lock.
6. Compare matched heights q=z/R. A hemisphere's nominal circular section radius is R*sqrt(1-q*q); a cylinder minus its inscribed cone has annulus outer radius R and hole radius q*R. Both nominal section areas are pi*R²*(1-q²). These are two sampled levels, not complete solids or a numerical volume proof. Display shelf separation is exaggerated independently for visibility. Mounting stems are excluded from theoretical areas. Circular boundaries use 96-sided polygon approximations, not exact analytic curves.
7. PNG/JPEG/SVG and text/icons are real 0.4–0.6 mm engravings in the front base apron at Y=-R-7 mm, away from spine sockets and shelf support. Bounded 20×20 threshold or grayscale height geometry retains sampled bright holes with continuous backing. This is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D reconstruction. Only engraving is offered to protect sheet thickness. STL stores no color.
8. ASCII/binary STL integrates its actual triangles by Boolean engraving. Confirm millimeter units. Limit 1,500 triangles; one convex closed outward-wound broad-flat-bottom solid. Concave/hollow/overhanging/self-intersecting/nonmanifold inputs are explicitly rejected, not repaired or replaced by a hull. Scale XY footprint and Z depth, rotate Z and place within the protected apron. Files stay local; portable JSON contains bounded payloads.
9. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables 865769, github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0. Measured 1.60 mm sheet, 4.50×1.700012 mm sockets, low carrier planes .21–.22 mm. Original Cavalieri reconstruction retains 4.50 mm tongues and measured default sheet with proposed calibrated sockets and stronger .4–.6 mm runners. No source artwork or raw mesh redistributed.
10. ZIP contains connected-card and seven clean-part STLs, current fits and these instructions. Connected-card and de-tabbed plate are different exports. Assembled/exploded views use the same solids; exports remain bed-flat. Computational mesh, fit, insertion, separation and runner checks are NOT physical print testing. Fit, glue strength, snipping force, sagging and durability have not been physically tested.
