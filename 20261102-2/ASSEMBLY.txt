Beat & Vernier — four-part sliding-scale kit card.
1. Print flat at 100% millimeter scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters. All parts/runners start at Z0; no supports intended. Adult small-parts craft, not a calibrated instrument.
2. Snip sixteen accessible runners, four per part, 1.6 mm outside contours. File nubs and elephant foot without altering tongues or slider edges. Individual STLs have no tabs.
3. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card, Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Measured1.60mm sheet and4.50×1.700012mm sockets; reconstructed sockets4.50×(sheet+allowance), tongues(4.50-allowance)×sheet. Allowance .10–.30mm TOTAL. Low source webs .21–.22mm redesigned as .4–.6mm bed-flat runners. No raw source STL bundled. Sliding assembly and scale geometry are original.
4. Print base, slider and a bridge first as a fit trial. Running clearance .20–.50mm is per side and above slider, distinct from joint allowance. Polish contact surfaces; do not force a tight fit. Material/printer calibration required.
5. Place slider on base, its engraved ticks beside the fixed front scale. BEFORE bonding, lower each bridge over the narrow neck into its socket pair, shoulders resting on base top. Verify free sliding to both wide handle stops. Remove bridges, apply compatible adhesive only to four tongues, then reseat square while keeping glue clear of slider and aperture. Allow to cure. Bridges are positively held only by cured adhesive, not friction.
6. Gently slide by the wide pads. One moving division spans (N-1)/N of a fixed division, so the next coincident tick advances by pitch/N. At centered position zero ticks align. Stroke ends at the actual wide-pad/bridge contact. Front margins and tick positions are physical engravings, not textures. No jaws, calibrated datum or metrology accuracy claimed.
7. Computational checks: each solid manifold/outward, one connected card, sixteen snips release four parts; all sliding positions collision-free, end stops and lift-out blocked, bridge insertion possible with slider already laid down; full bed footprints, clearances and engraving backing. No physical print, bond-strength, sliding-feel or measuring-accuracy tests performed. No switches, bearings, magnets or fasteners. Compatible adhesive required.
8. Local image/SVG2D-to-engraving uses a20×20 sampled height field with holes kept as uncut backing, not full3D reconstruction. Convex closed STL accepted for exact Boolean engraving; unsupported concave/open/overhanging meshes rejected explicitly. Artwork is isolated from scales and joints. STL contains no units or colours. ZIP includes connected card, four clean-part STLs and these instructions.
