Cycloid & Race — five-part comparison kit card.
1. Print flat at 100% mm scale, PLA/PETG, .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Print one rail and base first for fit calibration. No supports intended. Clearance .10–.35 mm TOTAL is printer/material dependent.
2. Clip TWENTY bed-aligned runners 1.5 mm outside clean edges: four per part. File nubs flush. Runners touch side webs below rolling edges and avoid all eight tongues. Wear eye protection; small parts and balls are choking hazards.
3. Place base flat. Lower the TWO identical CYCLOID rails into the front four sockets together, high ends on the left. Their shoulders rest on the base top and tongues terminate at the underside. Bond four joints using compatible adhesive; hold parallel until cured.
4. Lower TWO straight rails into the rear four sockets the same way. Bond four joints. Eight glue joints total; no friction/snap retention claim. Rails are paired with an inside gap smaller than the selected ball diameter. Do not load the model beyond gentle demonstration.
5. Supply two identical smooth 6–8 mm balls (not printed/included), a broad catch tray, snips and adhesive. Place balls on paired top edges a little forward of the steep cycloid start; release gently without pushing. Catch at the right exit. There is no automatic synchronized gate; compare qualitatively and repeat. Balls are not captive. Never aim at people.
6. Cycloid edge x=r(t-sin t), z=9+r(1+cos t), t=0..pi, r=span/pi. Straight edge connects the same endpoints. 64 segments approximate the cycloid. Ideal frictionless point-mass descent time differs; finite balls contact rail edges along offset paths, so this is NOT a precision brachistochrone timing instrument or a guaranteed winner. No physical rolling, timing, print or adhesive tests performed.
7. Personalization engraves only the protected front apron, max .6 mm deep with >=1 mm backing. PNG/JPEG/SVG and text/icons use a bounded 20x20 sampled threshold/grayscale surface, not full 3D reconstruction. Bright sampled holes remain unengraved. STL has no colors.
8. ASCII/binary STL uses the actual uploaded triangles as a Boolean engraving tool. Confirm mm units. One closed convex outward-wound flat-bottom solid, <=1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per source axis; concave/open/nonmanifold/overhanging/self-intersecting meshes explicitly rejected, no hull substitution or repair. Set axis before attaching; XY scale, Z depth, rotation and placement are independent.
9. Source: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0. Original reconstruction retains measured 1.60 mm default sheet and 4.50 mm tongues; calibrated sockets and .4–.6 mm stronger runners are new. No raw source mesh or spacecraft artwork redistributed.
10. ZIP includes connected-card, five clean-part STLs and these instructions. De-tabbed plate excludes the carrier. All exports are bed-flat; assembled/exploded positions use the same clean solids. Computational manifold, ball-gap, insertion, collision and runner-release checks are distinct from unperformed physical tests.
