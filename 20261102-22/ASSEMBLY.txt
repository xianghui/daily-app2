Lune & Proof — four-part mathematical aperture kit card.
1. Print flat at 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls; no supports intended. Print a panel/base fit first. Socket clearance .10–.35 mm TOTAL is printer/material dependent, not universal.
2. Clip SIXTEEN bed-aligned runners, four per part, 1.5 mm outside clean edges. File the stubs flush. Tabs join outer frame webs, not aperture tips or the six functional tongues. Small parts / snipping eye-protection caution.
3. Place the signature base flat. Identify panels by one/two/three recessed dots: lune A, lune B, right triangle. Stand them from LEFT to RIGHT. Lower both tongues of each panel simultaneously into the matching socket pair. Shoulders rest on the base top; tongues end flush with the underside.
4. Bond all SIX joints using a compatible adhesive and keep panels upright until cured. Clearance seats are not snap fits. Supports/hardware are not required; snips, file and adhesive are external consumables. The panels can be dry fitted before bonding; no strength or durability claim.
5. Compare the two crescent APERTURES with the triangular aperture. For triangle legs A,B, subtract the circumdisk (center A/2,B/2; radius hypot(A,B)/2) from disks on each leg. Hippocrates' theorem gives luneA + luneB = A*B/2. Curves use 256-sided polygons; actual computed aperture areas and approximation error are reported. The surrounding plaques are structural, not area-equivalent tiles. No tile-dissection or physical precision measurement claim.
6. Changing leg A/B changes the actual cut-through crescent/triangle regions. Rim and separation resize the frames/base. All three are displayed at the SAME physical scale, not normalized independently. Negative apertures preserve sharp theoretical tips without printing fragile positive tips.
7. Personalization engraves only the protected front base apron: 0.4–0.6 mm depth with >=1 mm backing. PNG/JPEG/SVG and text/icons use a bounded 20x20 sampled threshold/grayscale surface. Bright holes remain unengraved. This is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D photo reconstruction. STL does not store preview colors.
8. ASCII/binary STL uses the real uploaded triangles as a Boolean engraving tool. Explicitly confirm millimeter units. One closed convex outward-wound flat-bottom solid, <=1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per source axis. Concave/open/nonmanifold/overhanging/self-intersecting meshes are explicitly unsupported, not repaired or replaced by a hull. Choose up axis before attaching; XY size, Z depth, rotation and placement are independent.
9. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0. Read-only mesh inspection measured 1.60 mm sheet, 4.50 x 1.700 mm slots and low runners. Original parametric reconstruction retains sheet and tongue widths; calibrated slots, stronger .4–.6 mm runners, aperture mathematics, frame layouts and personalization are redesigned. No raw third-party mesh or spacecraft artwork bundled.
10. ZIP contains the connected card, four clean-part STLs and these instructions. Print plate is not the assembled overlap. Computational manifold/area/socket/insertion/runner checks do not imply a physical print, adhesive, fit or optical test; none has been performed.
