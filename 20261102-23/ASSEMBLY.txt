Pip & Assemble — a six-panel hollow display die, carried by twenty-four breakaway runners.
1. Print the connected card OR six clean parts flat, decorated sides UP, at 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls; no supports intended. Calibrate by printing ONE six-face panel and ONE two-face panel first and dry fitting their tongues/notches. Total mortise clearance .10–.35 mm is printer/material dependent. Do not force the joints.
2. Clip 24 bed-aligned runners, four per panel, 1.5 mm outside the clean edge; file stubs flush. Runner sites avoid the functional tongues, notches and pips. Keep snips pointed away and use eye protection; small parts are not for children.
3. Lay SIX face outside-down (pips to the table). Its notches face up. The TWO and FIVE walls are full width; THREE and FOUR are narrower and fit between them. Keep the decorated sides OUTWARD. Lower the two bottom tongues of each wall into the matching open edge notches. The bottom tongue ends are flush with the underside. Side walls have a deliberate 0.1–0.4 mm total corner seam. These seams and relieved corners are NOT a sealed vessel.
4. Bond the eight bottom tongue joints and wall corner seams with compatible adhesive; use light temporary tape or a right-angle jig to hold the walls square. Let these joints cure BEFORE closure. No flexing or blind snap assembly is required. Dry fit the ONE lid over all eight upper tongues, then lift it, apply adhesive at those joints, and lower it vertically. All sixteen tongue/mortise joints require adhesive; not a pressure fit or friction-retention claim. Keep glue out of artwork and wipe excess. Follow the adhesive maker's curing instructions.
5. Opposite faces are ONE/SIX, TWO/FIVE, THREE/FOUR (sum seven). Ordinary pips are recessed cylinders, not colors. Your import replaces the ONE central pip with a recessed personal mark; it is still the one face. Cube edge, corner seam, pips and panel corner relief all change actual geometry. Rounded/clipped panel corners make visible corner reveals, not globally filleted cube edges. It is an educational hollow die kit; rolling fairness, balance, adhesive strength and durability have NOT been tested. Do not use it for gambling or precision probability experiments.
6. Assembled view uses exact rigid transforms. Exploded view spreads the six parts to explain insertion; export always remains bed-flat. All loose-part and card STLs use the same current validated solids. STL stores no colors. A closed manifold PANEL does not mean the assembled six-panel cube is a watertight container.
7. Personalization is limited to the center of the ONE face: 0.4–0.6 mm recess, at least 1 mm backing. Image, SVG, text and icons use a bounded 20x20 sampled grayscale/threshold height surface Boolean-subtracted from the face. Bright holes retain full backing. This is 2D-to-relief, not full 3D photo reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Footprint, depth, crop, background, rotation and placement change the exported solid.
8. ASCII/binary STL is used as the actual Boolean engraving tool after explicit millimeter confirmation; STL contains no units. One closed convex outward-wound flat-bottom solid, <=1500 triangles, .2–250 mm per axis. Concave/open/nonmanifold/overhanging/self-intersecting meshes are explicitly unsupported, never repaired or replaced with a hull. Choose up axis before attachment. XY scale is proportional; Z scales independently to safe engraving depth. All uploads remain in the browser.
9. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card / Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0. Fresh read-only binary mesh inspection: 1.60 mm sheet, 4.50 x 1.700 mm sockets and low runners. This original reconstruction retains the measured sheet; the 4.50 mm socket length becomes our proposed tongue width, and sockets are redesigned as open-edge mortises for orthogonal cube closure, with explicit clearance and glue. New cube panels, recesses, 3x2 card layout, stronger .4–.6 mm runners and personal marks are not source spacecraft parts. Raw third-party mesh/artwork not bundled.
10. ZIP includes connected card, six clean-part STLs and this guide. Computational manifold, collision, insertion, face orientation, support-footprint and runner-release checks are separate from physical print/fit/rolling tests; NONE of the latter has been performed. External consumables: compatible adhesive, temporary tape, snips and file; no bearings, magnets or fasteners.
