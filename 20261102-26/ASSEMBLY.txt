Seism & Scribe — five-part relative-motion kit card.
1. Print decoration up at 100% mm scale. PLA/PETG; .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. All five sheets and runners start at Z=0. No supports intended. Snip the 20 thin runners 1.5 mm outside clean edges; file flush. Do not cut tongues, cord eyes or pencil bore.
2. Print the base and one portal first to calibrate the four 4.50 mm tongues. Sockets = (4.50 + total clearance) × (sheet + total clearance) mm. Fit is printer/material dependent. Bond portals into front and rear base seats, keep square and let adhesive cure.
3. Cut FOUR equal nonstretch cords, ≤0.8 mm diameter, plus knot allowance. Tie front and rear pairs through the 2.6 mm crossbar eyes and corresponding carriage eyes (x ±10 mm, y ±9 mm). The specified vertical drop plus 15 mm transverse offset sets the nominal straight length; knots change it. Level the carriage at the stated height. Knot above/below the sheets; do not glue moving cords.
4. Tape a small paper strip to the loose sled between its engraved lines. Lower a soft 6–8 mm pencil vertically through the 9 mm carriage bore and secure with removable tape. Adjust its tip to barely touch paper; no clamp or printed lead is provided. Four tied cords, pencil, paper, tape, snips, file and adhesive are required.
5. Set the sled flat between the portals. Move it gently left/right within the displayed stroke while the suspended pencil lags. Allow oscillation to settle; retune pencil contact if it drags. Keep away from the portals. It illustrates relative motion, NOT a calibrated seismometer or reliable measurement. The app does not simulate inertia, cord forces or pencil friction.
6. Flat sheet fit, cord suspension, physical writing, stability and adhesive strength have NOT been physically tested. Computational mesh/collision checks do not establish these. Small parts, adult maker use. STL stores no colour.
