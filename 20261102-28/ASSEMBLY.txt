Conchoid & Trace — three-part Nicomedes drawing kit card.
1. Print connected card bed-flat at 100% mm, PLA/PETG, .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm; runners .4–.6 mm. No supports intended. Print/fit/drawing accuracy have NOT been physically tested.
2. Snip TWELVE runners, 1.5 mm outside part edges; file flush. Release the windowed guide base, slotted tracing arm, and small rectangular guide shoe. The source carrier architecture comes from White Cat Kit Card by Mike Kasberg (CC BY 4.0), https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card / Printables 865769. Its measured 1.60 mm sheet and low .21–.22 mm runner regions inform an original CSG reconstruction; runners strengthened to .4–.6 mm. No raw reference STL or spacecraft artwork redistributed. Its measured 4.50 x 1.700 mm sockets are NOT relevant to this bolted linkage.
3. Hardware: TWO M3 x 16 mm screws, FOUR M3 nuts, SIX M3 washers, OD 7–8 mm; four adhesive feet at least 6 mm high; pencil, tape, paper and snips/file. No hardware-free alternative can retain this particular slotted drawing arm, so do not attempt to force-fit printed holes. M3 slots are 3 + clearance mm; round holes 3.40 mm. Use initial clearance .40 mm and print shoe/arm first to calibrate. Washer thickness is assumed about .8 mm; stack washers or change hardware to establish at least .8 mm arm/base clearance.
4. Tape paper on a flat desk. Fit four riser feet under base corners so screw heads/nuts do not touch paper. Insert fixed-pole M3 screw upward through the lower central round hole. Insert the second M3 screw upward through the shoe, then place shoe inside the long straight guide. Shoe is 8 x 6 mm; guide width is 6 + total clearance mm. Bottom screw head/washer must clear paper and slide beneath rail. Do not glue moving joints.
5. Put a washer/spacer above base at each screw, then lower the ARM so its long slot receives the fixed pole, and its round small hole receives the sliding screw. Add a top washer and pair of jam nuts to each screw. Lock nuts against each other while leaving the arm free to turn/slide with at least .8 mm above base. The washer OD must exceed slot width. Omitted hardware positions in preview are estimates, NOT an installation drawing; check the real hardware stack before operation.
6. Put the pencil through the large far-end bore, tip reaching paper through upper window. Use a removable tape sleeve to reduce play and maintain a light touch; an 8 mm bore is not a universal pencil fit. Hold base with one hand; gently sweep slider left/right without forcing endpoints. The guide intersection P=(x,a), pole O=(0,0), and pencil Q=P+b*P/|P| produce the outer conchoid branch. Precisely: Q=P+b P/|P|, or radial distance a/sin(theta)+b when theta is measured from X. Only x in [-travel,+travel] is supported; do not run into stops. The other conchoid branch is not implemented.
7. Base perimeter and cross rail remain outside pencil sweep, and arm slot contains pole over full supported travel. Remove arm vertically after undoing top nuts; no snap-fit or fatigue claim. Preview slider moves the real printed parts through this analytic pose but does not change print exports. Hardware, feet, paper and pencil are not printed or exported. The trace line in guide diagram is analytic, not a separately printed part.
8. Import PNG/JPEG/simple SVG or text/icons to protected lower-right base apron. Browser-only 20x20 sampled threshold/grayscale 2D relief, NOT full 3D photo reconstruction. Bright holes keep backing. Engraved depth .4–.6 mm, minimum 1 mm backing; no raised marks on this sheet. ASCII/binary STL is parsed and exact triangles used as Boolean engraving tool with proportional XY and independent Z depth. No convex hull substitution. Only single convex closed outward-wound broad flat-bottom solids <=1,500 triangles, .2–250 mm per axis, <=2 MB supported. Explicitly confirm mm. Concave/open/nonmanifold/self-intersecting/overhanging input rejected, not repaired. PNG/JPEG <=12 MP/6,000 px per side; simple SVG rasterized locally. Fit/crop, scale, rotate, place, replace/remove/reset alter exported solid geometry.
9. JSON/named designs retain bounded portable images/meshes and settings. Everything ZIP contains connected-card, clean part STLs, JSON and this guide. STL contains no preview colour. Geometry validation is not physical print testing or metrology certification. Small parts and sharp pencil: supervised use only.
