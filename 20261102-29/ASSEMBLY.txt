Reuleaux & Roll — three-part constant-width comparison kit card.
1. Print card bed-flat at 100% millimeters, PLA/PETG, .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, three walls. Floor/rotors 1.6–2.4 mm, tray maximum twice that thickness. Low runners .4–.6 mm. No supports, hardware or adhesive intended. Physical printing and rolling feel NOT tested.
2. Snip TWELVE runners 1.3 mm outside bounding edges. File stubs flush without gouging rotor arcs. For best dimensional accuracy, use clean individual rotor STLs instead: tab filing can spoil constant width. A small printed object is not a certified gauge.
3. Place the tray flat. Lower Reuleaux rotor into left half of channel and circle gauge into right half. No force fit: this is an open-top drop-in assembly. Circle and arc rotor have equal nominal width. Turn each with a finger; the curved polygon spans the same guide separation at every angle even though its centre shifts. Keep each rotor in its own half of the channel; pieces are intentionally loose and can be lifted out.
4. Three, five or seven equal-radius arc intersections create distinct regular odd Reuleaux polygons. Disc radius equals chosen constant width; disc-centre circumradius W/(2 cos(pi/(2n))). Cylinder faceting is bounded at 360 sides; sampled width error <=.02 mm computationally, not a manufacturing accuracy claim. The circle is a true geometric control, not a second puzzle tile. Preview centres each rotor between parallel rails using actual mesh support extrema; this shows fitting poses, NOT no-slip rolling dynamics. Export always uses bed-flat current parts, never overlapping assembled geometry.
5. Guide gap = rotor width + .30–.60 mm TOTAL clearance. Start .40 mm for PLA and print clean rotor/tray first. Calibrate extrusion, elephant foot and friction; clearances are not universal. No pressing or captive retention is claimed. Minimum apron backing is 1 mm; art never enters functional guide surfaces or rotor perimeters. Source White Cat Kit Card by Mike Kasberg, CC BY 4.0: https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card / Printables 865769. Inspected source sheet 1.60 mm and low runner .21–.22 mm architecture adapted; strengthened runners and entirely new tray/rotors. Source 4.50 x 1.700 mm sockets are not used. No source mesh redistributed.
6. Personalize the apron with PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text/icons or exact verified ASCII/binary STL Boolean engraving. Images become a local bounded 20x20 threshold/grayscale recessed height field, not automatic 3D reconstruction. Bright holes keep backing. .4–.6 mm depth. STL has no units: confirm mm, orient up-axis, scale proportional XY and independent Z engraving depth, rotate/place inside safe zone. Only one convex closed outward-wound broad flat-bottom solid, <=1,500 triangles, <=2 MB, .2–250 mm per axis supported. Unsupported concavity, open surfaces, intersections, overhangs are rejected, not repaired or convex-hulled.
7. Image fit/crop, threshold, background and transforms alter actual exported solid. JSON and named saves retain bounded portable input; no personal file is uploaded. Everything ZIP includes real connected card, three clean part STLs, design JSON and this guide. STL stores no preview colours. Computational manifold/clearance checks do not establish print strength, friction or physical operation. Snips and small parts require care.
