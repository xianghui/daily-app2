Bend & Compare — six-part three-point beam-comparison kit card.
1. Print every part bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale, PETG preferred, .16–.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters, identical settings for all beams. No supports intended. This is an adult small-part demonstration, not a load-bearing tool or calibrated test rig.
2. Snip 24 runners (four per part), 1.6 mm outside contours; file nubs. Preserve four support tongues and all three notches in each support. Clean-part STLs omit runners. Never bend strips to remove tabs.
3. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card, Printables 865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY 4.0. Measured sheet 1.60 mm, socket loops 4.50×1.700012 mm, low carrier webs .21–.22 mm. Reconstructed slots (sheet+allowance)×4.50, tongues (4.50-allowance)×sheet; allowance .10–.30 mm TOTAL, not per side. New bed-aligned .4–.6 mm runners, original beam/cradle assembly, no raw reference mesh bundled.
4. Dry-fit both cradles downward in the base sockets, with the narrow notch on the same side. Shoulder stops rest on the base top. Print base plus one cradle first as a calibration pair. Clean elephant foot and adjust fit; bond four seats square with compatible adhesive and let cure. Loose adhesive joints, not snap locks.
5. Lower each beam from above into its matching pair of notches: narrow lane Y18, middle Y30, wide Y42 mm. Ends overhang the support centres by 6 mm. Strips rest freely, NOT clamped or captive. Lift out freely to replace. Keep the base on a level desk and do not invert while strips are loaded.
6. Gently press one beam at its midpoint, release, then compare the others using similar tiny hand pressure. Start below 0.5 mm displacement; guide limit is min(.8,span/100) mm, NOT a strength guarantee. Stop immediately at whitening, permanent set or cracking. Never hang weights, use as a spring launcher, or force a brittle PLA strip. PETG response, creep, print direction and fatigue vary. No physical printing, force, fatigue, deflection or strength test performed.
7. For ideal equal-material, small-deflection, simply-supported rectangular beams, k≈4 E b t³/L³. Width ratios give nominal stiffness ratios only; E and real hand force are unknown, and finite supports are not exact point supports. No absolute force or measured deflection is claimed. Thickness and span change all strips together, widths differ by 2 mm. Base clearance greatly exceeds the gentle displacement guide.
8. Every intended part is a closed manifold solid. Tests check card bounds, 24 safe snips, six released parts, socket insertion paths, beam lowering paths, notches, end margins and assembled nonintersection. Preview shows the UNDEFORMED assembly; exploded spacing is illustrative. No fake force simulation.
9. Local PNG/JPEG/SVG become sampled 20×20 base engravings, not 3D reconstruction. Exact supported convex STL imports become real Boolean cuts in the protected base patch; concave/open meshes rejected, never replaced by a hull. Backing stays at least 1 mm, away from sockets and beams. STL has no unit metadata or preview colours. ZIP includes card, six clean-part STLs and this guide. Adhesive required; no weights, bearings, switches, magnets or fasteners required.
