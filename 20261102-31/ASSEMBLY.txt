Hanoi & Transfer — eight-part physical transfer kit card.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% millimeters in PLA/PETG with .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers and three walls. All pieces one sheet thick, 1.6–2.4 mm. No supports or metal hardware. Physical printing and fit have NOT been tested. Small parts and snips require care.
2. Snip THIRTY-TWO low runners 1.3 mm outside part bounding edges. File remaining stubs flush. Do not gouge ring bores, peg shoulders or rectangular tongues. Tabs touch only outside edges. The connected card holds all eight real parts; clean part exports omit the runners.
3. Dry-fit each of three peg tongues into a board socket. Source-derived socket length 4.50 mm; tongue width 4.50 minus selected .30–.60 mm clearance. Socket thickness = sheet plus clearance. Tongue length equals sheet, so its end is flush with board underside and shoulder seats on top. These are deliberately loose glue seats, NOT press fits or self-locks. Print one clean peg and board first, compensate elephant foot and calibrate your material. Apply a little plastic-compatible adhesive in sockets, hold pegs upright and cure fully. Keep adhesive away from ring surfaces.
4. Lower four rings over left peg, largest first. Rings rest on 3 mm high peg shoulders, not on the floor. Hole diameter = 6 plus twice clearance; always greater than the 4.50-by-sheet blade diagonal. Peg centres separated by largest ring diameter plus 4 mm prevent adjacent stacks colliding. Rings have no retention: lift clear of peg tip, translate above all tips, lower onto another peg. No sliding through or bending around pegs.
5. Move one top ring at a time; never put a larger ring on a smaller one. Transfer the full stack to the right peg in fifteen moves. Assembly preview chooses one of sixteen exact resting states; it is not a browser game or a continuous physics simulation. Exploded view is illustrative only. All STL exports remain bed-flat. Four diameters run from 16 mm to chosen 32–38 mm maximum. Peg height 22–32 mm leaves tested stack headroom. These are geometric fit checks, not force/friction/adhesive strength tests.
6. Personalize the protected front board apron with text, bundled icons, PNG/JPEG/simple SVG or verified ASCII/binary STL Boolean engraving. 20x20 threshold/grayscale conversion is 2D-to-relief, NOT automatic full 3D reconstruction. Bright holes preserve backing. .4–.6 mm recess leaves at least 1 mm backing and stays out of sockets. STL input supports only one closed convex outward-wound broad flat-bottom solid, <=1500 triangles, <=2 MB, .2–250 mm per axis. Confirm mm units, select up-axis, scale, rotate and place. Unsupported meshes are rejected, never convex-hulled or silently repaired.
7. JSON and named saves retain bounded portable input; all local processing, no uploads. Everything ZIP contains real connected card, eight clean part STLs, design JSON and this guide. STL does not store preview colours. Reference White Cat Kit Card by Mike Kasberg, CC BY4.0, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card / Printables865769. Actual source sheet1.60 mm, socket4.50x1.700 mm and low runner.21–.22 mm measured. Strengthened runners.4–.6 mm and new original ring/peg/board geometry; no raw source mesh redistributed.
