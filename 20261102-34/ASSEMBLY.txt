Harmonic & Overhang — eight-part gravity-stacking kit card.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% mm, PLA/PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, 100% infill and at least three walls. All parts one 1.6–2.4 mm sheet thick. Use identical settings/material for all five planks; no supports or hardware intended. Small parts; adult snipping supervision. Physical printing and stability have NOT been tested.
2. Snip 32 low runners 1.3 mm outside part edges. File stubs completely flush without changing plank length or thickness. Clean part STLs omit runners. Card has a real connected carrier, not a loose plate. Five planks are IDENTICAL; do not engrave, sand unevenly, add labels, or change their mass. Personalization only on fixed base.
3. Stand the two supports transversely in the four board sockets. Each support has two short shouldered tongues; the tongue tips end flush with the board underside and shoulders seat on top. 4.50 mm source socket length retained. Tongue width is 4.50 minus selected .30–.60 mm total clearance, socket narrow dimension sheet plus clearance. Loose adhesive seats, NOT self-retaining press fits. Dry-fit one support/base first, compensate elephant foot and calibrate filament. Bond with plastic-compatible adhesive and hold perfectly vertical, equal-height, until cured.
4. The forward support right edge defines X=0. Arrange planks bottom first, across both supports, with their long axes perpendicular to the supports. Bottom right edge extends q*L/10 beyond X=0. Successive right-edge offsets above are q*L/8, q*L/6, q*L/4, q*L/2; q=.60–.85 is the harmonic fraction. Top total reach q*L*(1+1/2+1/3+1/4+1/5)/2. Use a ruler in mm. UI reports exact current offsets. No adhesive between planks. No captive retention: they are meant to lift off. Build gently on a level stationary surface; never push sideways or treat it as load-bearing furniture.
5. Exact assembled view uses calculated offsets and contacts; the offset-fraction preview can retract toward a centred stack. No physics simulation. At every supported interface, the uniform-mass upper-stack centre lies inside the contact polygon; margin is reported. This is a static geometric calculation, NOT a promise against vibration, warping, friction, slicer mass imbalance or tipping. Exploded view is illustrative; exports always bed-flat. Keep hands ready to catch loose planks. No added load.
6. Personalize the protected base signature zone (25,-12 mm) using text, bundled icons, PNG/JPEG/simple SVG or verified ASCII/binary STL Boolean engraving. 20x20 threshold/grayscale sampled conversion is 2D-to-relief, NOT reconstruction of full 3D from a photo. Bright holes retain solid backing. .4–.6 mm engraving keeps >=1 mm backing and cannot touch sockets or balancing pieces. STL: one closed convex outward-wound broad flat-bottom solid, <=1500 triangles, <=2 MB, .2–250 mm per axis. Explicit mm confirmation, up-axis, footprint scaling, Z depth, rotation and placement. Unsupported meshes rejected; no hull substitution or repair.
7. JSON/named saves include bounded input payloads. All processing local; no uploads. ZIP has connected card, eight clean STLs, design JSON and this guide. STL stores no preview colours.
