Cipher & Turn — three-part alphabet dial kit card.
1. Print bed-flat at 100% mm, PLA/PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, at least three walls. Sheet 1.6–2.4 mm; alphabet engraving .4 mm. No supports or hardware intended. Plastic-compatible adhesive REQUIRED for axle/base joint. Small parts; adult snipping supervision. Physical printing and turning have NOT been tested.
2. Snip 12 low runners 1.8 mm outside part edges, then file stubs flush. Card has an actual Boolean-unioned carrier and detachable parts, not a loose plate. Keep the axle tongue, round bearing bore, head underside and dial faces clean. Axle tabs touch head sides, never the socket tongue. Clean STLs omit runners.
3. BEFORE bonding: turn the T axle upright, pass its narrow tongue and 8 mm spine DOWN through the dial bore from above; the integrated 14 mm head stays above. Hold the dial lifted while seating the tongue down in the base socket. Shoulders stop on the base, tongue ends flush underneath. Confirm the dial rests freely on the base and clears the head by .30–.60 mm. Rotate through a full turn dry. Then apply adhesive ONLY in the base socket, seat the axle square, and prevent adhesive reaching the dial or bearing. Cure fully. Do not attempt to force the head through the bore. The head positively retains the dial only after the axle is securely bonded.
4. Source socket length 4.50 mm retained. Tongue width=4.50 minus .30–.60 mm total socket clearance; socket narrow dimension=sheet plus clearance. These are loose adhesive seats, NOT self-retaining press fits. Bearing hole radius=hypot(8,sheet)/2 plus selected radial clearance. This encloses the complete rectangular spine at all angles. The minimum bore-envelope gap is NOT uniform radial play: the rectangular axle permits more sideways float in Y than X. Align letters by engraved ticks, not precision detents. UI reports the circular-envelope float estimates; polygonal bore is slightly smaller. Calibrate a clean dial and axle first; elephant foot may require light deburring. Printer/material-dependent gaps are not universal fits.
5. Align inner A with the desired outer key letter. Read each inner plaintext letter against the outer ciphertext letter in the same radial sector; reverse to decode. Preview shift k rotates inner dial clockwise by k*360/26 degrees, so inner A meets outer letter k. A at shift 3 meets D; HELLO becomes KHOOR. This is an educational substitution cipher, NOT secure encryption. All 26 letters are actual .4 mm recesses using an original 5x7 bitmap alphabet; .60–.80 mm strokes. Optional paint wiped into recesses may improve contrast. Rotation preview is an analytic pose, not physical friction simulation; exports stay bed-flat.
6. Personalize only the protected fixed-base corner (R+8,-R-8 mm), keep-in radius6.5 mm; .4–.6 mm engraving keeps >=1 mm backing and cannot touch the dial sweep, labels or socket. Text, bundled icons, PNG/JPEG/simple SVG or verified ASCII/binary STL are Boolean engraving. 20x20 threshold/grayscale sampled height conversion is 2D-to-relief, NOT full 3D from a photo. Bright holes retain backing. STL one closed convex outward-wound broad flat-bottom solid, <=1500 triangles, <=2 MB, .2–250 mm per axis. Confirm mm, choose up-axis before attaching; set footprint, depth, Z rotation and XY placement. Unsupported concavity is rejected, never replaced by a hull or silently repaired.
7. JSON and local named designs include bounded portable input payloads. All processing local, no uploads. ZIP contains connected card, three clean part STLs, current design JSON and guide. STL carries no colours. Reference White Cat Kit Card by Mike Kasberg, CC BY4.0, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card / Printables865769.
