Vacant & Glide — nine-part sliding-tile fidget kit card.
1. Print card face up at 100% mm; PLA/PETG, .4 mm nozzle, .16–.20 mm layers, 3+ walls. Board has a solid floor and integral raised rim; eight one-sheet numbered tiles lie bed-flat. No hardware, glue or supports intended. Small parts; adult supervision when cutting runners. Physical printing and sliding have NOT been tested.
2. Snip 36 low runners 1.8 mm outside part edges. File the stubs flush, deburr tile bottoms and wipe the pocket clean. Do not cut into board rim. Clean individual STL exports omit the frame and runners.
3. Lift each clean tile into the pocket from above. Place 1–3 in the top row, 4–6 middle, 7–8 bottom-left/middle, lower right vacant. Only slide an orthogonally adjacent tile into the vacancy. The maker preview demonstrates tile8 moving one pitch right; it is NOT a browser puzzle game. Slide others by hand on the physical object. Lift out to reset; no permanent bond needed.
4. Tiles are deliberately REMOVABLE and NOT captive. Keep board level: rim prevents horizontal escape but tiles fall out if inverted. No retention latch, precision detent or security claim. This is not suitable for small children. Sliding gap .30–.60 mm is proposed printer/material-dependent clearance, not a universal fit. Print a clean board plus two tiles to calibrate; dry-fit and adjust gap rather than rescaling the slicer. Do not force elephant-foot edges.
5. Sheet1.6–2.4 mm; continuous floor same thickness; rim top at2*sheet + rim-rise, leaving .4–1.2 mm fence above assembled tiles. Tile width=pitch−gap, pocket width=3*pitch+gap: neighbor and outer-edge gaps both equal selected gap in the shown aligned pose. No separated pieces or overlap are exported as a union; every part is Boolean solid, card is one connected solid.
6. Board apron artwork only, safe radius6.5 mm at (0,−boardHeight/2+9). Engraving .4–.6 mm preserves >=1 mm backing and cannot obstruct pocket. Text, bundled icons, simple SVG and PNG/JPEG convert locally through a20x20 thresholded or grayscale height field, then Boolean-cut; holes retain material. This is2D-to-relief, NOT full3D photo reconstruction. Imported closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-bottom ASCII/binary STL is used as the exact Boolean cutter; concavity is rejected, never replaced by a hull. Confirm mm; choose up-axis then attach, scale/rotate/place. Files <=2 MB, <=1500 STL triangles; images <=12MP/6000px per side.
7. Everything ZIP contains connected card, board, eight numbered tile STLs, portable JSON and this guide. STL has no colours or units metadata; use millimetres. All processing is local, no uploads. Reference: White Cat Kit Card by Mike Kasberg, CC BY4.0, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card / Printables865769. Actual measured sheet1.60 mm and low runner planes.21–.22 mm informed original carrier construction. Proposed runners strengthened to.4–.6 mm; no source sockets are imposed on this slot-free kit. Original board, eight tiles, engraving and clearance redesign; raw source not bundled. Geometry, assembly and runner checks are computational, not physical print/friction testing.
