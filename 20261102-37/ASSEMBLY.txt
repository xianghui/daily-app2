Curry & Gap — five-part missing-square dissection kit card.
1. Print flat at100% mm; PLA/PETG, .4mm nozzle, .16–.20mm layers,3+walls. One gridded board with integral rim, two right triangles and two stepped polyominoes. No glue, hardware or supports intended. No physical printing, friction or legibility testing has been performed.
2. Snip20 low runners1.8mm outside each actual piece boundary; file stubs flush. Do not cut into the triangular tips or steps. Clean per-part STLs omit runners. Small parts; adult supervision when cutting. Triangular tips remain pointed; handle gently and deburr. Inset shifts each edge inward without rounding the mathematical corners.
3. Coordinates in unit cells from the lower-left of the13×5 board: LayoutA — 8×3 triangle(0,0),5×2 triangle(8,3),7-cell step(8,0),8-cell step(8,1). LayoutB — 8×3 triangle(5,2),5×2 triangle(0,0),7-cell step(5,0),8-cell step(8,0). All retain their printed orientation. Lift pieces completely before changing layouts. A one-cell square opening appears at(7,1) inB. Keep board level; pieces are removable, not captive.
4. The trick is NOT a gain/loss of material: slopes3/8 and2/5 differ. Outer profiles have opposite shallow kinks, not one common straight hypotenuse. The four nominal areas are12+5+7+8=32 cells. The imagined13×5 right triangle is32.5 cells. Difference between the two bent outlines is1cell, matching the deliberate hole. Generated pieces are inset by gap/2 for real printer clearance, so their true areas are slightly smaller; the nominal proof ignores these seams. Grid grooves.25mm deep, .3mm wide remain outside the art zone.
5. Unit pitch5–7mm, sheet1.6–2.4mm, proposed total piece clearance.15–.35mm; these are printer/material-dependent starting values, not universal fits. Print a clean board and one step first; deburr elephant foot. Floor at grid>=1.35mm. Rim top2*sheet+rise. No forced press fit or compliant joint.
6. Apron engraving only, radius6.5mm at(0,-boardHeight/2+9), depth.4–.6mm and >=1mm backing. PNG/JPEG/SVG, text and bundled icons convert through a20×20 threshold/grayscale height field then Boolean subtraction. Holes retain material. This is2D-to-relief, NOT full3D reconstruction. ASCII/binary STL: exact source Boolean cutter, one outward closed convex broad-flat-bottom solid; concave/open/overhanging input rejected, never hull-replaced. Explicit mm confirmation; axis, scale, rotation and placement. Files<=2MB; images<=12MP/6000px side; meshes<=1500triangles. All files local, never uploaded.
7. Everything ZIP contains connected card, five cleanSTLs, portable JSON and this guide. STL has no colour/unit metadata: use mm.
