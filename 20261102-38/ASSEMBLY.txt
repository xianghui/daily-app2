Taut & Parabola — three-part taut-string drawing kit card.
1. Print flat at100% mm: PLA/PETG, .4mm nozzle, .16–.20mm layers,3walls. The focus pad is1mm thick; ruler and guide floor1.6–2.4mm. Guide fence is2*sheet+.6mm high. No supports intended. Physical printing, cord tracking, friction and drawing accuracy have NOT been tested.
2. Snip12 runners1.8mm away from each actual part edge; file stubs flush, especially ruler anchor and fence. Do not cut the shoe, cord notch or focus hole. Individual STLs are clean. Keep sharp tools and small parts away from children.
3. Tape the fixed guide flat to paper, apron towards you; its straight fence face isy=-16. Drop the sliding square shoe onto the guide floor atZ=sheet, gently maintaining the proposed running gap. The long ruler extends away from you. This is an open hand-guided fence, NOT a captive bearing or snap assembly. Use stroke marks; do not slide beyond ±halfStroke.
4. Focus pad: tape it to paper with hole center atx=0,y=focus+gap measured from the virtual directrixy=gap. Feed <=.3mm nonstretch thread through its1.8mm hole and tape the tail beneath. The pad is1mm thick so the ruler can pass above it with >=.6mm underpass. Keep knots below the pad, the cord exit recessed and any tape out of the sliding path. No printed peg protrudes through the moving ruler.
5. Tie/tape the other thread end at the ruler's open left-edge notch(x=0 locally,y=anchorHeight+gap). Set free string length between ideal anchor points equal to anchorHeight using a ruler, not overall printed piece length. Run thread UNDER the elevated ruler where it crosses; the low focus anchor and notch must remain clear. Hold a fine pencil against the ruler's LEFT straight edge, catching the string at the pencil tip. Gently tension and slide. Never force the cord over/through solid plastic. Cord, pencil, paper and tape are external requirements; no metal hardware or glue.
6. Coordinates relative to directrix: y=(x²+f²)/(2f), vertexf/2. String pathH-y+sqrt(x²+(y-f)²)=H. UI thread/pencil lines are ideal preview-only geometry, not printable parts. Zero-radius planar equations do not account for pencil radius, finite eye width, cord thickness or friction. This is an approximate educational tracing kit, not a precision drafting instrument. Print the clean ruler and guide first; deburr and calibrate running gap. Default free cord65mm, focus15mm, stroke±26mm.
7. PNG/JPEG/SVG, text or bundled icons become a20×20 threshold/grayscale relief Boolean-subtracted into the protected guide apron at(0,-28), radius6.5mm, depth.4–.6mm, >=1mm backing. Holes retain material. This is2D-to-relief, NOT3D reconstruction. Exact verified convex ASCII/binary STL becomes an engraving cutter, never a hull replacement. Confirm mm; orient, scale, rotate and place. Concave/open/nonmanifold/unsupported meshes are explicitly rejected. All files stay local. No STL colours.
8. Everything ZIP contains connected card, three cleanSTLs, portable designJSON and this guide.
