Borromean & Bound — six-piece collectively interlinked rectangular-ring kit card.
1. Print the card or clean parts bed-flat at 100% millimeter scale. PLA or PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters, no supports intended. Check bed size: card210–250 ×180–220 mm. Clean-part STL downloads fit smaller beds. This is a small-part mathematical display, not a load-bearing chain or child toy.
2. Snip 24 accessible runners, four per part, 1.6 mm beyond contours; file nubs. Preserve six loop-end tongues and six closure sockets. Never bend a U loop to remove tabs. Each loop uses its U-shaped part and one perpendicular closure bar. A’s central bar has the personal engraving.
3. Reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card, Printables865769; https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card ; CC BY4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Actual STL measured:1.60 mm sheet,4.50×1.700012 mm socket loops,0.21–0.22 mm low webs. New CSG reconstruction, no raw source mesh redistributed. Proposed total fit0.10–0.30 mm: socket4.50×(sheet+fit); tongue(4.50-fit)×sheet. Runners widened/thickened to0.8–1.4×0.4–0.6 mm. Calibration and adhesive required, not a universal press fit.
4. Dry-fit an A loop/bar pair first. Clean elephant foot. Both broad shoulders stop against the near closure-bar face, with tongue tips emerging1.2 mm beyond the far face. A compatible adhesive, not friction, holds all six seats. Never bond the three different rings together.
5. Keep ALL three closure bars off. Place A in the XY plane, long span along X, open mouth toward+Y. Raise B from below along+Z, long span along Y and open mouth toward+Z. Feed C from+Y toward the centre along−Y, long span along Z and open mouth toward+X. The long/short inequality leaves both insertion corridors clear. The U loops are now orthogonal at a common centre.
6. Bring each bar to its adjacent open mouth, not through a distant ring: A atY=r+5 mm then slide−Y by5; B atZ=r+5 then slide−Z; C atX=r+5 then slide−X. The bar’s16 mm direction stands normal to its own U sheet. For each bar, align both4.50 mm sockets with its two tongues. Test the entire structure dry, bond all six seats, and let cure.
7. Each pair is geometrically separable with the third ring absent, while all three together have alternating cyclic over/under crossings: the rectangular Borromean link. It is not a serial chain and not three pairwise-linked rings. Closed loops must not be forced apart. Opening/removing a closure breaks the topological condition; glue removal is not guaranteed nondestructive. This is a static hand-held topology model, not a simulated mechanism or load test.
8. Tests check all six assembled parts separately, nonintersections, each assembly corridor, six socket/shoulder seats, ring-pair separation with third absent, canonical centreline disk-piercing pattern and pairwise linking numbers, card bounds,24 safe snips and closed manifolds. Preview exploded offsets are illustrative only; STL always uses bed-flat print geometry. Mesh checks do not prove physical assembly, adhesive strength, durability or printer fit; none physically tested.
9. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG → local20×20 thresholded or grayscale engraved height mesh on A’s closure bar. Bright/transparent holes retain original material beneath; depth0.4–0.6 mm, backing≥1 mm, safe radius6.5 mm away from sockets. This is2D-to-relief, not full3D reconstruction. Text and bundled icons use the same local raster-to-solid path.
10. ASCII/binary STL → strict closed, outward, connected, convex, broad-flat-bottom validation (≤1500 triangles). Explicitly confirm mm, select up axis, XY footprint, depth, Z rotation and placement. Exact mesh Boolean-subtracted as an engraving; concavities/open meshes are rejected, NEVER replaced by a hull. All files stay local, portable JSON carries bounded image/mesh payloads. STL carries no preview colours. ZIP contains connected card, six clean-piece STLs and these notes. Adhesive required; no bearings, switches, magnets or fasteners required.
