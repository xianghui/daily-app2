Chiral & Mirror — three-part tetrahedral handedness kit card.
1. Print card or clean parts flat at 100% mm scale, PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers and three perimeters. No supports intended. Card190–240 ×170–220 mm. Small mathematical display, not a chemical model with atomic radii or a load-bearing object.
2. Snip12 runners (four per part)1.6 mm beyond the contours; file nubs. Keep bottom tongues and half-lap slots intact. Print one dock/frame pair first to calibrate. Sockets4.50×(sheet+total allowance), tongues(4.50−allowance)×sheet. Sheet1.6–2.4 mm; total allowance0.10–0.30 mm depends on printer/material. Adhesive required, no universal press-fit claim.
3. Stand upper-ray frame in XZ with its open half-slot upward. Stand lower-ray frame in YZ with its half-slot downward. Start lower frame above upper and slide down until both bottom edges align. The opposite half slots form a cross-lap with total gap equal to allowance. No bending or forced snap.
4. Lower the crossed pair together into the four dock sockets; broad shoulders rest on dock top. Tips emerge1.2 mm beneath the dock. Set the dock across two scraps while inserting and bonding, then file protruding tips if a table-flat bottom is desired. IMPORTANT assembled model rests on four tongue tips, not on the dock underside. Optional adhesive fills gaps. Dry-fit first, then bond four seats and centre lap. No extra hardware.
5. The numbered dimple nodes are the four substituent sites:1/2 on upper XZ rays,3/4 on lower YZ rays. Radius r20–28 mm, height offset r/√2; all six centre-to-node angles109.471°. Reversing3/4 creates the opposite handedness. Print the opposite-hand preset/setting and compare by rotation: the four labels cannot superimpose. The extra rectangular frame is structural scaffolding, not extra chemical bonds. No R/S chemical priority naming claimed.

This is2D-to-relief, not full3D photo reconstruction. Text and icons follow the same conversion. Sharp pixel contours are approximated by triangulated samples, not vector tracing.

Confirm mm (STL has no units), set up-axis before import, footprint, engraving depth, Z rotation and placement. Exact Boolean subtraction, not a convex-hull replacement. Unsupported concavity/open/self-intersecting meshes rejected. All files local. Portable JSON includes bounded asset payloads. Preview colours are not in STL.

Mesh/card/slot/assembly-path checks and analytical tetrahedral angles are computational checks; no physical printing, fit, adhesive strength or assembly tested.
