Bone & Product — seven-part Napier multiplication-rod kit card.
1. Print card or clean parts flat at 100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .16–.20 mm layers, .4 mm nozzle. Numeral strokes .48 mm, engraving .4 mm; inspect slicer for legibility. No supports intended. Not a precision calculating instrument.
2. Snip 28 runners, four per piece, 1.6 mm beyond contours. File nubs without damaging four fence tongues or the numerical faces. Card 220–250 ×160–210 mm; impossible combinations block export. Clean-part exports suit smaller beds.
3. Calibrate one fence/tray seat first. Each socket is4.50×(sheet+total allowance) mm; tongue(4.50−allowance)×sheet. Default1.60mm sheet, allowance0.10–0.30mm TOTAL, not per side. FDM/material dependent, no universal press-fit claim. Bond both fences into four sockets after checking upright alignment. Tips project1.2mm under tray, so rest tray on scraps while bonding and file tips if a flush underside is required.
4. Lower the four rods independently from above with headers at the back. Each rod has digit0–9 in the header and nine two-digit partial products. Multiplication rows run1–9 from back to front. Rods remain loose, with0.3–0.7mm nominal gaps between adjacent rods and at fences. The open ends allow removal/reordering; do not glue rods or claim captive retention.
5. Select row m. In each diagonally divided cell, tens are upper-left and units lower-right. Starting at right, read the exposed units triangle; then sum units from the left neighboring cell with tens from the right cell, carrying as needed; finish at far-left tens. Example1684×3: row products03|18|24|12 give5052. Leading zero headers allowed. Actual digits and all36 product cells are engraved geometry, not textures.

Tray, loose arithmetic rods, engraved products, fences and frame packing are new. Raw source not redistributed.
