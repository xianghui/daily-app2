Flag & Signal — six-part semaphore kit card.
1. Print all parts/card flat at 100% millimeter scale in PLA/PETG, .16–.20mm layers, .4mm nozzle, three perimeters. No supports intended. Computational checks only, no physical print, motion, strength or assembly testing.
2. Snip24runners, four per part, 1.6mm beyond contours; clean nubs and first-layer expansion. Six clean-part exports omit runners. Carrier is a real connected Boolean union, not loose parts near a frame.
3. Calibrate the source-derived sheet joints first. Measured reference:1.60mm sheet and4.50×1.700012mm sockets. Socket4.50×(sheet+allowance); tongue(4.50−allowance)×sheet. Allowance.10–.30mm TOTAL, not per side, printer/material dependent. Print an individual keeper/axle to check fit, then one mast foot/base socket. No universal tolerance claim.
4. Lower mast feet into base sockets, shoulders on deck and tips flush underneath. Dry-fit then bond both fixed feet. Supply compatible adhesive; no bearings/magnets/fasteners required. Without adhesive this is a loose-fit dry assembly, not a retained working mechanism.
5. Lay front arm over the headed shaft, slide shaft through rectangular mast aperture, add rear arm, then keeper. Keep each arm a clearance away from mast and outer stop. Shaft length =4sheet+4clearance. Bond shaft ONLY inside mast aperture and keeper ONLY at shaft tip, flush to outer keeper face. Mark shaft tip with a pencil or use temporary tape to establish that flush position. Do not slide keeper inward or glue arms. Keeper adhesive is essential: no positive snap is claimed. Cured head/keeper obstruct arm withdrawal, with radial overlap computationally checked. Keep loads low.
6. Arms independently rotate in separated planes about a rectangular shaft inside larger circular bores. Bore radius=half shaft diagonal+running clearance. Radial/axial clearance.25–.45mm are proposed FDM starts. Rotate by hand; physical friction/feel untested. Larger flags must remain above base throughout rotation; parameter ranges and CSG sweep check this. Front/rear arms deliberately occupy different planes; this is a desktop semaphore representation, not a calibrated or official maritime signal device. No spring or detent is claimed.
7. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG become local20×20 sampled threshold/grayscale engravings in the base at(0,−12),radius6.5mm; depth.4–.6mm,backing≥1mm. Bright holes retain full backing. 2D-to-relief, not full3D reconstruction or exact vector tracing. Text and searchable icons use same real mesh method. All files stay local.
8. ASCII/binary STL exact solid Boolean engraving; ≤1500triangles,2MB,one closed outward connected convex broad-flat-bottom solid only. Confirm mm; select up axis before attachment, scale/rotate/place. Open/nonmanifold/concave/cavity/unsupported meshes rejected without repair or hull substitution. Portable bounded JSON/local designs include imported assets.
9. Connected card, assembled/exploded, clean plate and isolated views share final meshes. Exploded positions are illustrative; export is printable flat geometry, no preview colours.
