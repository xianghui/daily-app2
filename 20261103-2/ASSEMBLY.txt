Involute & Unwind — three-part cord-unwrapping drawing kit card.
1. Print card flat Z=0 at100% mm, PLA/PETG, .4mm nozzle, .16–.20mm layers,3+walls. No supports intended. All geometry computationally checked; physical printing, friction and drawing accuracy NOT tested. Small parts; adult supervision.
2. Snip12 low runners1.8mm outside each part and file stubs FLUSH. The circular former MUST have a smooth circular rim. Clean part STLs avoid tab scars. Do not cut the grip tongue, pencil bore or shoe socket.
3. Slide upright-grip tongue down into pencil-shoe slot until shoulder seats; bond with suitable adhesive. Default sheet1.60mm, slot4.50x1.70mm, tongue4.40x1.60mm. Clearance .10–.30mm is total proposed printer-dependent allowance, not per-side. Print shoe and grip first for calibration. No snap/friction retention claimed.
4. Required external consumables: fine low-stretch cord <=0.5mm diameter, a pencil smaller than chosen bore7–9mm, paper, tape and grip adhesive. Tape former underside firmly to large paper. Thread cord through radial tie hole and knot; lead its short end to the rim at the engraved start tick, wrap clockwise once around the smooth edge, keeping cord at sheet midheight. Tie free end DIRECTLY around pencil shaft near tip, through the shoe bore, with no slack. Begin with tangency 120deg counterclockwise from the radial start tick, remaining cord wrapped clockwise from the tie eye to tangency, and free tangent length R x 2.094 (41.9–62.8mm). Total working cord around former plus free tangent is approximately one circumference. Extra knot tails are excluded. Do not start near zero unwrap: the shoe would hit the former. Pencil tip remains on paper; shoe is hand-held, not an offset cord anchor. A small collar of tape can stop cord riding up pencil; do not tape it to the shoe edge.
5. Keeping cord taut and former fixed, move pencil along the illustrated involute to unwrap (tangency advances counterclockwise) cord without slipping. Ideal point-string path from (R,0) is x=R(cos t+t sin t), y=R(sin t-t cos t). Radius and actual free leader length affect curve. The 192-sided rim approximates a circle; finite cord/pencil radii, slack, friction and leader length cause error. NOT a precision gear-profile tool. Use fine cord, compare a short arc with plotted points. Rewrap to repeat. Grip is merely an ergonomic vertical guide; do not twist it around the pencil.
6. Assembled preview translates shoe to ideal involute point and seats grip. Pose120–240deg is illustrative ONLY; cord/pencil/tape are external and omitted, curve drawn in diagram is theoretical. The shoe rotates freely by hand; keep it outside former. Exploded view lifts grip16mm; it is not a separate print pose. All STL exports use identical checked bed-flat part geometry.
7. PNG/JPEG/SVG, text and icons become20x20 threshold/grayscale height-map Boolean engravings at the fixed former center, .4–.6mm deep, >=1mm backing. White holes retain solid material. This is2D-to-relief, not full3D reconstruction. Exact outward closed convex broad-flat-bottom imported STL is subtracted as a cutter, never convex-hulled. Concave/open/nonmanifold/overhanging files explicitly unsupported. Confirm mm and up-axis, scale/rotate/place within protected6.5mm radius. Files<=2MB; images<=12MP/6000px side; STL<=1500 triangles. All processing local; portable JSON includes bounded assets. STL has no colours or unit metadata.
