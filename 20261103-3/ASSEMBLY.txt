Jump & Vacancy — 33-part flat-pack peg-solitaire kit card. This is a physical puzzle generator, NOT a playable browser game.
1. Print the connected card bed-flat Z=0 at100% millimeter scale. PLA/PETG, .4mm nozzle, .16–.20mm layers, 3+ walls. No supports intended. Sheet1.6–2.4mm. Use clean-part downloads to avoid tab scars; Everything ZIP contains every part, design JSON and this guide. Geometry and fit have computational checks, NOT physical print or play testing.
2. Snip132 low breakaway runners1.8mm outside the33 parts. File all remaining runners completely flush, especially the tongues at the bottom of each peg. Do not shorten the actual tongue, widen a slot or cut the peg shoulder. Adult supervision, small parts/choking hazard.
3. Lay the board flat on a level table. The33 rectangular sockets form an English cross. Insert32 pegs from above, leaving the middle socket empty. A peg's broad shoulder rests on the board and its full-sheet tongue ends flush with the underside. Pegs are loose and lift out; they are not retained when inverted. No glue, hardware or external material required.
4. Default source-derived fit: sheet1.60mm, socket4.50x1.70mm, tongue4.40x1.60mm. Clearance.10–.30mm is TOTAL, not per side. Print the board plus one peg first as a fit coupon. FDM extrusion width, elephant foot, material and humidity affect fit. If tight, increase clearance. No universal fit or strength claim. Do not force thin tongues.
5. To play physically, jump one peg horizontally or vertically over ONE adjacent occupied peg into the immediately following empty socket, then remove the jumped peg. Diagonal jumps and moves into occupied/nonexistent sockets are not allowed. Aim to leave one peg. The center vacancy has a verified33-site/31-jump logical solution recorded in validation evidence; the browser does not simulate the game. Pitch12–16mm and8mm-wide grips give4–8mm nominal transverse finger separation. Small boards may require a gentle pinch grip.
6. Flat card, de-tabbed plate, assembled and exploded views use the same checked geometry. Assembled seats32 pegs in sockets. Exploded raises them18mm for illustration only. Isolated board and each numbered peg are available. Exports ALWAYS remain bed-flat; no assembled overlaps are exported. Peg numbering identifies files only, not gameplay labels.
7. PNG/JPEG/SVG/text/icons become20x20 threshold or grayscale Boolean engravings on the lower signature apron, .4–.6mm deep, at least1mm backing. Bright image holes retain uncut backing; this is sampled2D-to-relief, not exact vector tracing or full3D photo reconstruction. Placement is bounded to a6.5mm radius separated from all33 sockets. STL imports use the EXACT parsed mesh as an engraving cutter: single closed outward-wound convex broad-flat-base solid only, <=1500triangles, .2–250mm each axis. Concave/open/nonmanifold/self-intersecting/overhanging meshes are explicitly rejected, never replaced by a convex hull. Confirm mm, choose up-axis before attaching, scale XY/engraving depth independently, rotate and place. All imports<=2MB; raster images<=12MP/6000px per side. Files stay local; bounded payloads travel in JSON/named saves. STL contains no colors or unit metadata.
8. Reference: Mike Kasberg White Cat Kit Card, Printables865769, https://github.com/mkasberg/white-cat-kit-card, CC BY4.0. Reparsed actual30,616-triangle binary mesh; measured1.60mm sheet,4.50x1.70mm slots and .21–.22mm low runner planes. Reconstruction uses stronger.4–.6mm runners,33 real puzzle sockets and32 shouldered removable pegs. Original spacecraft geometry/raw STL not redistributed. Source defects are documented, not repaired. Full measurements and deviations are in reference-analysis.json.
