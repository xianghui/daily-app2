Sag & Sphere — five-part three-contact curvature demonstrator kit card.
1. Print connected card bed-flat, 100% mm scale. PLA/PETG, 0.4mm nozzle, .16–.20mm layers, 3+ walls. No supports intended. Use clean-part files to avoid tab scars. Physical printing and accuracy are untested.
2. Snip 20 low runners 1.8mm outside the five parts. File all stubs flush, especially the three upper foot tongues and probe shaft. Do NOT shorten contact tips or file away scale ticks. Small parts; adult supervision.
3. Dry-fit three identical feet from BELOW the deck. Their shoulders stop at the deck underside; full-sheet tongues end flush with the deck TOP. Glue only these three static seats after calibration. Hold deck level while adhesive cures. Required external material: a small amount of suitable adhesive. No bearings, screws or magnets.
4. Lower the narrow end of the probe from ABOVE through the central slot, scale facing you. It slides freely and is NOT captive. Support the probe with your fingers while moving the instrument; do not invert. Top handle is only an upper insertion stop, not a lock. Keep glue away from the central slot.
5. Calibrate on a flat rigid surface. Three narrow foot lands and the probe tip touch the same plane; the LONG zero tick should meet the deck TOP edge. Printer extrusion and first layers can shift this datum. Mark any offset; do not claim submillimeter accuracy. Print deck + one foot + probe as a fit coupon before full card. Total XY clearance .10–.30mm is printer/material dependent.
6. Place the tripod gently on a broad rigid curved surface, lower the probe to touch it without force, and read the approximate sag at the deck top. Each tick is 1mm; the long middle tick is zero. Deeper-than-feet sag is POSITIVE (zero tick drops below deck). Convex bulge gives negative sag. The short tip lands are .8mm wide by sheet thickness, not ideal points. Use only on nonfragile surfaces; this is an educational model, not calibrated metrology or medical equipment.
7. For an ideal sphere R=(a²+s²)/(2|s|), where a is contact-circle radius, NOT triangle side length. At s=0 curvature is flat/infinite radius. The three contacts are 120 degrees apart, chord sqrt(3)*a. Probe preview is limited to ±4mm; it does not simulate surface contact or force. Real surface radius and tip geometry introduce error. Slide travel has geometric clearance checks, not physical friction testing.
8. Assembled/exploded/isolated/de-tabbed views use these exact solids. Exploded feet move down14mm and probe up28mm for explanation only. Export is ALWAYS bed-flat. Card contains real20 removable runners, not loose parts alongside a frame.
9. PNG/JPEG/simple SVG, text and six bundled icons become Boolean engraved artwork in the deck apron, away from all four sockets; bright holes retain backing. This is sampled20x20 2D relief, not full 3D reconstruction. Closed convex ASCII/binary STL may be transformed and Boolean-subtracted as an exact custom recess. Unsupported concave/open/overhanging/multipart meshes are rejected, never replaced by a convex hull. STL has no unit metadata: confirm millimeter coordinates. Every file stays in your browser. Portable JSON includes bounded assets. STL stores geometry, NOT preview colors.
10. Structural reference: Mike Kasberg, White Cat Kit Card, Printables865769, CC BY4.0. Original sheet/socket/carrier reconstruction; no raw source mesh redistributed. Default sheet1.60mm and socket4.50x1.70mm are measured source dimensions; tongue4.40x1.60mm and .4–.6mm strengthened runners are proposed adaptations. No spacecraft shape or physical-test claim.
