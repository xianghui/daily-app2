## Limits — not physical validation

No physical print, actual fit, glue bond, tension, long-term strength or measurement accuracy test performed. All clearances are printer/material-dependent starting points. Straight feet require adhesive; inset is loose. Six threads/tape/adhesive are external supplies, not supplied meshes. Crossing heights in preview are estimated, not a force/sag simulation. Source STL has nonwatertight artifacts and was used only as a design reference, never silently repaired or redistributed. Imported concave or otherwise unsupported STL is explicitly rejected, not hulled. The bounded20×20 relief method is not a full3D reconstruction or exact vector trace. STL does not retain preview colours.
