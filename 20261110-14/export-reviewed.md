# Community newsletter — Criterion Canvas

Scores and weights are user-supplied judgments, not AI measurements. All criteria must use comparable 0–10 higher-is-better anchored scales. Cost must already be translated to a benefit score; raw costs are not accepted. No automatic score inference or normalization. Weighted sums assume additive, compensatory preferences. A complete matrix needs exactly one approved note per option–criterion cell; duplicate evidence is never silently averaged. Held notes survive but do not count. Leave-one-criterion-out comparisons are sensitivity scenarios, not probabilities or proof of a best real-world choice.
