## Limits

Semantic similarity is not supplier identity, contract interpretation or payment authorization. Tiny fixtures do not measure real-world accuracy; unrelated text and overlapping supplier categories can mislink. Single currency, whole-day ages, last eligible discount-day payment, simple interest, no tax/weekend/late-fee/liquidity handling. Offline scope is a warmed open page only. CPU Chrome tested; physical phones and other engines unverified. Model registry revision is not pinned. No bank access, actual transfers, automatic scheduling or measured savings claim.
