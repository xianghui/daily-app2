## Important limits

Fixed monthly-interest model only; no lender connections or payments. No variable minimums, daily compounding, fees or new borrowing. Every account mapping and number needs human verification. Similarity is not identity verification, confidence or financial advice. No universal optimality or accuracy claim. Upstream model revision is not pinned. Cache eviction needs internet. At most eight unique accounts; batch triage supports forty notes but duplicates cannot silently double-count a debt. Long-horizon balances are explicit; an extreme growing-balance bound fails safely rather than emitting imprecise arithmetic.
