## Limitations deliberately exposed

Unrelated instruction-like text still receives a nearest activity: semantic retrieval always picks a candidate. It remains pending, never executes instructions, and can be held. No confidence percentages are shown. Negation, vague notes, overlapping activity meanings and unseen activities require human checking. No timestamp extraction or causal process diagnosis is claimed. The small synthetic fixtures are regression checks, not field performance or accuracy guarantees.
