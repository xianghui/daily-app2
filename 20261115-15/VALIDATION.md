## Limitations

Semantic nearest-neighbor matching is not task identity verification, duration measurement, confidence or process safety. Negation/unrelated/near-identical methods can mislink. Planner assumes identical stations, indivisible tasks and supplied deterministic timings; ignores real tool/travel/setup/zoning/ergonomic constraints. Missing/held methods mean incomplete scope. No external dispatch, manufacturing controls or uploads.
