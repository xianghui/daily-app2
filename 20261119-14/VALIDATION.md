## Limitations

Semantic nearest-neighbor inference is not identity proof, negation reasoning or confidence. Constant hazard and failure-free fixed recovery are strong assumptions; no uncertainty intervals or workload-rate predictions are validated. Correlated failures, finite jobs and changing save costs are not modeled. User-provided measurements/caps require human verification; the app never changes settings.
