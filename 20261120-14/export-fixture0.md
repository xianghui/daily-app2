# Replicate Commons — independent-unit review worksheet

Synthetic nonclinical surface-test scores. Each registered specimen is an independent unit; repeated readings are technical repeats.
Unit: score; repeat-range tolerance: 2

Descriptive only: technical repeats are not additional independent specimens. Equal-specimen means are appropriate only when each registered specimen should have equal weight and readings use the same measurement protocol/unit. This tool does not establish independence, random sampling, treatment effects, significance or uncertainty intervals. Complete roster required for equal-specimen summaries; pooled baselines can remain available on partial data and are explicitly labeled. Holds can bias results. Leave-one-unit-out contrasts are sensitivity scenarios, not confidence intervals. Single readings have no assessed repeat range; zero range is not proof of repeatability. Tolerance is your recheck threshold, not an AI safety limit.
