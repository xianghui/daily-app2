# Chroma Ledger — color recheck worksheet

Synthetic textile swatches. Compare only under the stated shared measurement condition; inspect physical samples separately.
Measurement condition: Synthetic CIELAB readings; D50, 2-degree observer, same instrument / geometry / backing for targets and samples.

CIELAB ΔE76 is Euclidean distance in supplied Lab coordinates, NOT CIEDE2000, a perceptual-uniformity guarantee, calibration, color rendering or a production-release certificate. Supplied thresholds are user policy, not industry defaults. Targets and samples must use the same illuminant, observer, geometry, instrument settings and backing. The shared condition is an operator assertion, not verified by AI. No inference of color from names, photos or screen pixels. No spectral/metamerism, uncertainty, gloss or substrate correction. Lightness-only baseline deliberately ignores a* and b*; extra recheck flags are computed, not measured accuracy gains. AI retrieves target meaning, not the correct formulation or physical acceptability. Holds stay in audit and are excluded from checks.
