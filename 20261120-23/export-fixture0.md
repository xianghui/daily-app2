# Climate Ledger — weather-normalized review

Heating diaries — synthetic daily observations, same occupancy and meter boundary assumed.

Descriptive fitted comparison, not verified savings or a causal retrofit effect. Equal daily exposure, stable occupancy, consistent meter boundaries and a suitable linear degree-day relationship are assumptions. HDD/CDD and temperature bases are supplied, not inferred. No weather or meter service is contacted. RMSE is in-sample, not forecast accuracy. Different sites are never summed.
