# Factor Grove — factorial review

Workshop drying — synthetic balanced trials. Same response unit, independent comparable runs assumed.

Descriptive full-factorial contrasts only, not causal proof or statistical significance. Low/high signs and response units are supplied, not inferred. All conditions must be present with equal replication; held trials are excluded but retained. Independent comparable runs, no confounding and consistent measurement are assumptions. Additive RMSE is across cell means; repeat RMS is within cells, denominator N, not an uncertainty interval. Interactions depend on all other factors. Never automatically change a process from this worksheet.
