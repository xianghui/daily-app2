## Scope / limitations

This is identity reconciliation and a conditional approximation worksheet, not live queue control. Similarity is not semantic truth or reliable negation detection; unfamiliar/adversarial descriptions require correction/hold. The instruction-like unrelated fixture stays inert and is held. No inference upload, tool authority or credentials are requested. Sample adequacy, stationarity, independence and one-server FIFO assumptions require human judgment. Zero sample variation cannot establish zero future waiting; estimates are not service-inclusive turnaround, tails, guarantees or measured capacity. M/M/1 baseline can be higher or lower. Conditional target-rate ceiling at zero variation is a limiting capacity, not a stable equality operating point.
