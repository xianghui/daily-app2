# Crossover Cove — reviewed process trial

Synthetic archive retrieval trial. Lower minutes are better. Unequal AB/BA group sizes.
Unit: minutes

Descriptive two-period AB/BA contrasts, not causal proof or significance tests. Assumes common additive period effects, stable method effects, comparable units and no carryover. Sequence differences can reflect selection; subject-specific period effects are not resolved. A two-period design cannot separately identify carryover. Randomization, washout, measurement quality and applicability require human verification. Do not use for clinical or safety decisions. No extrapolated savings.
