## Limits
Embeddings retrieve similarities even for unrelated/instruction-like reports; they cannot certify cause relevance/completeness, interpret negation reliably, validate supplied outcomes/costs or diagnose faults. Single-cause and deterministic-check assumptions are essential. The unpinned model and cache eviction can cause new downloads. No guarantee of test safety, real-world diagnostic accuracy, mobile memory performance, offline startup or realized productivity improvement.
