## Scope limits

Lexical worksheet only: no archive parsing, directory entries, symlink/hardlink or existing-filesystem inspection, no Unicode Windows case-folding/normalization, and no extraction guarantee. Windows is deliberately ASCII-only. Independent normalization checks establish behavior for tested cases, not complete platform semantics. Model fixture scores are not general accuracy or measured time savings.
