## Not tested / limitations

Physical phones, other browser engines, cold offline navigation, representative event-language accuracy, real equipment datasets, informative-censoring detection, unit independence and causal interpretations are unverified. No confidence intervals, extrapolation or multi-cohort treatment comparisons. Model main revision is unpinned. Holds change the cohort; review is essential. The retirement-as-censoring baseline can target a different scientific question and is not presented as universally invalid.
