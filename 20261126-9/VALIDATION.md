## Arithmetic scope and limits

Rational minor-unit fractions are not rounded journal entries. No fees/taxes/options/variable rent, accounting classification, dates or legal interpretation. Rates up to 50% per period are bounds tests, not realistic recommendations. The baseline is computed all-arrears with current reviewed terms; it is neither truth nor a savings claim.
