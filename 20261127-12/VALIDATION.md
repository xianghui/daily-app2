## Known limitations

Nearest-reference matching forces a choice even for unrelated text (instruction-like probe chose NORMAL); human review is essential. Negated telephoto probe chose TELE, but this does not establish general negation handling. Optical arithmetic is a thin-lens model, not a measured photograph. CoC, subject distances, principal-plane convention, close-focus effects and diffraction must be independently assessed. No camera/device/real lens performance validation, representative semantic accuracy, cold offline reload, physical-phone or other-engine tests. No measured time saving or fabricated confidence percentages. No digest sent.
