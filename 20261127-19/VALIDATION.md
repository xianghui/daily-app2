## Limits

Single-row centered horizontal **angular** model only. Angular overlap is not pixel-area overlap, vertical coverage or successful stitching. Real lens/sensor data, level entrance-pupil rotation, motion, distortion and parallax require field verification. No physical photography, stitching, real-camera control, representative semantic accuracy, low-memory device performance or cold-offline startup tested. The four easy examples are not universal accuracy evidence.
