## Limitations

Synthetic coefficients, integer daily input and floor rounding, uniform rainfall, empty initial tank, discard every wet day rather than storm event, inflow before overflow before demand, no leaks/evaporation/intra-day timing. No prediction of future rainfall or seasonal reliability. Wrong semantic links remain possible and require review. Physical phones, low-memory devices, non-Chrome engines, cache eviction, cold offline startup, real roof measurements, water quality and engineering outcomes are unverified. No digest sent.
