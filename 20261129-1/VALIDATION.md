## Limits

Not live carrier pricing, not actual measured assembly weight and not a carrier compliance engine. No folds, rigidity, bindings, glue, destinations, tracking, minimum dimensions, discounts, taxes or scarce packaging stock modeled. Human measurements, applicable offers and carrier rules remain required. MiniLM fails negation and can mislink unfamiliar terms. No source upload, equipment control, mailing or postage purchase.
